# Short-Form Content Pillars — Star Laundry Boyolali

These are the content pillars adapted specifically for short-form video (Instagram Reels and TikTok). Each pillar defines what the video looks like, example concepts, hooks, ideal length, best platform, and posting frequency.

---

## 1. Edukasi Laundry

**What it is:** Educational content that teaches viewers about laundry, fabric care, stain removal, and clothing maintenance. Positions Star Laundry as the expert.

**What it looks like as a reel:**
- Talking head (owner/staff) explaining a tip directly to camera
- Step-by-step demonstration with close-up shots
- "Myth vs Fact" format with text overlays
- Before/after of a stain removal technique

**Example Concepts:**
1. "3 kesalahan nyuci yang bikin baju cepet rusak"
2. "Cara hilangkan noda kopi dari kemeja putih"
3. "Bedanya deterjen cair vs bubuk — mana yang lebih bagus?"
4. "Kenapa baju kamu bau apek walau udah dicuci"
5. "Suhu air panas vs dingin — kapan pakai yang mana?"

**Hooks:**
- "Kamu pasti pernah salah ini waktu nyuci..."
- "Ternyata selama ini kita cuci baju salah..."
- "Jangan pernah pakai air panas buat cuci baju ini!"

**Ideal Length:** 30-45 seconds
**Best Platform:** TikTok (educational content thrives here), cross-post to IG
**Posting Frequency:** 2-3x per week

---

## 2. Satisfying / Transformation

**What it is:** Visually satisfying content showing the transformation from dirty to clean. ASMR-adjacent. Designed for saves and shares.

**What it looks like as a reel:**
- Side-by-side or sequential before/after
- Time-lapse of washing machine cycle
- Satisfying folding sequences
- Close-up of steam ironing wrinkled → crisp fabric
- Packing montage with neat stacks

**Example Concepts:**
1. "Tumpukan kotor → rapi dalam 2 jam" (full transformation)
2. "Satisfying setrika kemeja" (ironing ASMR)
3. "Watch this stain disappear" (noda hilang close-up)
4. "From chaos to clean" (messy pile → organized delivery bag)
5. "Sprei kusut → hotel-level crisp" (bedsheet ironing)

**Hooks:**
- "Tunggu sampai akhir..." (wait for it)
- "Dari segini jadi segini" (visual before/after)
- "Ini yang paling satisfying hari ini"

**Ideal Length:** 15-30 seconds
**Best Platform:** Instagram Reels (aesthetic content performs well), also strong on TikTok
**Posting Frequency:** 2x per week

---

## 3. POV / Relatable

**What it is:** First-person perspective content that makes the target audience feel seen. Uses humor and everyday situations related to laundry struggles.

**What it looks like as a reel:**
- POV format with text overlay describing the scenario
- Staff/owner acting out a relatable situation
- Split screen "expectation vs reality"
- "Tell me you're ___ without telling me" format

**Example Concepts:**
1. "POV: Anak kos yang udah 2 minggu ga nyuci"
2. "POV: Nyokap suruh nyuci semua gordyn jelang Lebaran"
3. "POV: Kamu baru sadar besok interview tapi kemeja kusut semua"
4. "POV: Hujan seminggu nonstop dan cucian numpuk"
5. "POV: Pertama kali ke coin laundry dan bingung pencet apa"

**Hooks:**
- "Kalau kamu pernah kayak gini, kamu butuh Star Laundry..."
- "POV: [scenario yang relatable]"
- "Ini kamu banget pasti..."

**Ideal Length:** 15-30 seconds
**Best Platform:** TikTok (relatable/POV culture is strongest here)
**Posting Frequency:** 2x per week

---

## 4. Behind the Scene (BTS)

**What it is:** Authentic behind-the-scenes content showing daily operations, staff activities, and the care that goes into every order. Builds trust and humanizes the brand.

**What it looks like as a reel:**
- "A day in the life" of Star Laundry
- Staff sorting, washing, folding with real-time commentary
- Morning opening routine
- How an order goes from drop-off to delivery
- Machine maintenance and cleaning routines

**Example Concepts:**
1. "Sehari di Star Laundry — dari jam 7 pagi sampai tutup"
2. "Ini proses orderan kamu dari awal sampai akhir"
3. "Cara kami pisahin baju biar ga luntur"
4. "Rutin bersih-bersih mesin setiap hari"
5. "Packing orderan kamu — ini kenapa rapi banget"

**Hooks:**
- "Pernah penasaran gimana laundry proses baju kamu?"
- "Ini yang terjadi di balik layar Star Laundry..."
- "Banyak yang ga tau ternyata kami lakuin ini setiap hari"

**Ideal Length:** 30-60 seconds
**Best Platform:** Both (IG for polished BTS, TikTok for raw/authentic BTS)
**Posting Frequency:** 1-2x per week

---

## 5. Promo Flash

**What it is:** Short, punchy promotional content announcing discounts, special offers, new services, or limited-time deals. Always paired with a clear CTA.

**What it looks like as a reel:**
- Bold text announcements with energetic music
- Quick montage of the service being promoted
- "Breaking news" style announcement
- Countdown or urgency-driven format
- Staff announcing the promo directly to camera

**Example Concepts:**
1. "PROMO: Cuci setrika Rp 7.000/kg minggu ini aja!"
2. "Baru! Layanan antar jemput gratis radius 3 km"
3. "Flash sale: Cuci gordyn diskon 20% — cuma 3 hari"
4. "First time? Gratis cuci 3 kg pertama kamu!"
5. "Promo Ramadan: Bersih sambut Lebaran, sprei diskon 20%"

**Hooks:**
- "STOP SCROLL — promo ini cuma sampai [tanggal]!"
- "Kabar baik buat kamu yang di Boyolali..."
- "Siapa yang mau cuci gratis? Baca sampai akhir!"

**Ideal Length:** 15-20 seconds
**Best Platform:** Instagram Reels (promo content is more accepted here)
**Posting Frequency:** 1-2x per week (never more than 2, or feed feels spammy)

---

## 6. Customer Proof

**What it is:** Social proof content featuring real customer reactions, testimonials, reviews, and user-generated content. Builds trust and credibility.

**What it looks like as a reel:**
- Customer reaction when receiving clean laundry
- Screenshot compilation of WhatsApp testimonials (with permission)
- "Customer of the day" feature
- Before/after of a customer's order
- Google Maps review highlights

**Example Concepts:**
1. "Reaksi customer pertama kali pakai Star Laundry"
2. "Apa kata customer tentang Star Laundry" (review compilation)
3. "Customer kirim 15 kg — lihat hasilnya!"
4. "Ibu ini tadinya ragu, sekarang langganan tiap minggu"
5. "Google review 5 bintang — makasih semuanya!"

**Hooks:**
- "Ini reaksi jujur customer kami..."
- "Kenapa mereka balik lagi ke Star Laundry?"
- "Review ini bikin kami semangat pagi-pagi"

**Ideal Length:** 15-30 seconds
**Best Platform:** Both (IG for polished testimonials, TikTok for raw reactions)
**Posting Frequency:** 1x per week

---

## 7. Challenge / Interaktif

**What it is:** Interactive content designed to drive engagement through challenges, polls, questions, and audience participation. Optimized for comments and shares.

**What it looks like as a reel:**
- Staff challenge (speed folding, kg guessing game)
- "Tebak berapa KG?" audience participation
- This-or-that polls in video format
- "Challenge accepted" trend adaptations
- Duet/stitch invitations

**Example Concepts:**
1. "Tebak berapa KG tumpukan ini?" (audience guesses in comments)
2. "Speed fold challenge — staff A vs staff B"
3. "Tim cuci sendiri vs tim laundry — kamu yang mana?"
4. "Challenge: lipat kaos dalam 3 detik"
5. "Stitch this: tunjukin tumpukan cucian kamu!"

**Hooks:**
- "Coba tebak berapa KG ini..."
- "Siapa yang lebih cepat?"
- "Kamu tim mana? Comment di bawah!"

**Ideal Length:** 15-30 seconds
**Best Platform:** TikTok (engagement/comment culture is stronger)
**Posting Frequency:** 1x per week
