## RECORDING NOTES

### Angle & Shot
- Hook: Medium shot talent di depan kos / masuk outlet
- Beat 1: Wide shot area mesin + medium shot talent
- Beat 2: Close-up mesin, tangan masukin baju, tombol start, text overlay harga
- Beat 3: Medium shot talent duduk santai, close-up HP, wide shot mesin selesai
- CTA: Static end card

### Lighting
- Gunakan lighting natural outlet + lampu mesin cuci area
- Pastikan text overlay terbaca jelas

### Tips Syuting
- Baju kotor: siapkan baju warna-warni biar visual menarik
- Tas laundry: yang gembung biar keliatan banyak
- Expression talent: harus natural, jangan acting berlebihan
- Ambil 2-3 take per scene buat opsi editing
