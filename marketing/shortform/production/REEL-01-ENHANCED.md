## 2. STEP 1: ELEVENLABS -- Generate Voiceover

### Instructions

1. Go to: **https://elevenlabs.io/app/speech-synthesis**
2. Log in or create a free account
3. Select **Text to Speech** from the left sidebar

### Voice Settings

- **Language:** Indonesian
- **Voice:** Click "Voices" and search for **"Sari"** (Indonesian female) or **"Cinta"**. If neither is available, pick any Indonesian female voice that sounds friendly and energetic.
- **Model:** Multilingual v2
- **Stability:** 50%
- **Clarity + Similarity Enhancement:** 75%
- **Style Exaggeration:** 0%
- **Speaker Boost:** ON

### Text to Paste

Copy and paste this EXACT text into the text box. The ellipsis (...) and commas create natural pauses:

```
Baru pulang mudik? Isi koper pasti kayak gini...

Baju kotor numpuk dari perjalanan... males banget nyuci sendiri.

Eits, tenang! Star Laundry udah buka lagi mulai hari ini! Tinggal drop off, beres.

Bersih, wangi, rapi... tinggal pakai lagi!

Cucian kamu aman, rapi, siap diambil.

WA kita sekarang ya, Kak!
```

### Export

1. Click **"Generate"**
2. Listen to the preview -- make sure the pacing is natural and approximately 28-30 seconds
3. If too fast, add more periods (...) between sentences for longer pauses
4. If too slow, remove some pauses
5. Click the **download icon** next to the generated audio
6. Choose **MP3** format
7. Save as: `reel-01-voiceover.mp3`

### Output

One MP3 file, approximately 28-30 seconds. Keep this file ready for assembly.

## 3. STEP 2: KLING AI -- Animate Real Photos (Image-to-Video)

### General Instructions for All Clips

1. Go to: **https://klingai.com**
2. Log in or create a free account
3. Click **"AI Videos"** in the top menu
4. Select **"Image to Video"** mode
5. For each clip below:
   - Upload the specified photo

- **Mode:** Standard (free tier) or Professional (if available)
- **Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 (vertical)
- **Duration:** 5 seconds (default)
- **Camera Control:** As specified per clip
- **Negative Prompt (paste for all clips):**

```
blurry, distorted faces, morphing objects, unnatural movement, flickering, glitch, low quality, watermark text changes
```

## 6. STEP 5: ASSEMBLY

### Option A: CapCut Assembly (Recommended -- Easier)

**Download CapCut:** https://capcut.com (free, available on desktop and mobile)

#### Step-by-Step Assembly

**1. Create New Project**
- Open CapCut
- Click **"New Project"**
- Set project aspect ratio to **9:16**
- Set frame rate to **30fps**

**2. Import All Files**
Import these files into the media panel:
- `clip-3a-suitcase-open.mp4` (from Nano Banana)
- `clip-2a-dirty-laundry.mp4` (from Kling AI)
- `clip-3b-dirty-to-clean-transition.mp4` (from Nano Banana, optional)
- `clip-2b-machines-running.mp4` (from Kling AI)
- `Mesin.mp4` (real video from assets)
- `clip-2c-folding-clothes.mp4` (from Kling AI)
- `Orang lagi lipat pakaian.mp4` (real video from assets)
- `clip-2d-clean-shelves.mp4` (from Kling AI)
- `cta-endcard.png` (from Canva)
- `reel-01-voiceover.mp3` (from ElevenLabs)

**3. Arrange on Timeline**

Drag clips to the video track in this exact order and trim to these durations:

| Timeline Position | Clip | Duration | Voiceover Line |
|-------------------|------|----------|----------------|
| 0:00 - 0:05 | `clip-3a-suitcase-open.mp4` | 5.0s | "Baru pulang mudik? Isi koper pasti kayak gini..." |
| 0:05 - 0:10 | `clip-2a-dirty-laundry.mp4` | 5.0s | "Baju kotor numpuk dari perjalanan... males banget nyuci sendiri." |
| 0:10 - 0:14 | `clip-2b-machines-running.mp4` | 4.0s | "Eits, tenang! Star Laundry udah buka lagi..." |
| 0:14 - 0:18 | `Mesin.mp4` (real video) | 4.0s | "...mulai hari ini! Tinggal drop off, beres." |
| 0:18 - 0:21 | `clip-2c-folding-clothes.mp4` | 3.0s | "Bersih, wangi, rapi..." |
| 0:21 - 0:24 | `Orang lagi lipat pakaian.mp4` (real video) | 3.0s | "...tinggal pakai lagi!" |
| 0:24 - 0:28 | `clip-2d-clean-shelves.mp4` | 4.0s | "Cucian kamu aman, rapi, siap diambil." |
| 0:28 - 0:30 | `cta-endcard.png` | 2.0s | "WA kita sekarang ya, Kak!" |

**Note:** If you made the optional transition clip (3B), insert it between 0:10-0:11 as a 1-second overlay.

**4. Add Voiceover**
- Drag `reel-01-voiceover.mp3` to the audio track
- Align it to start at 0:00
- Make sure the audio lines up with the scenes. Adjust clip durations if needed to match the voiceover timing.

**5. Add Text Overlays**

Click **"Text"** in the toolbar, then **"Add text"** for each:

| Time | Text | Font | Size | Color | Position |
|------|------|------|------|-------|----------|
| 0:00 - 0:05 | `Baru pulang mudik?` | Montserrat Bold | 48 | #FFFFFF with black outline | Bottom center |
| 0:05 - 0:10 | `Males nyuci sendiri...` | Montserrat Bold | 48 | #FFFFFF with black outline | Bottom center |
| 0:10 - 0:18 | `Star Laundry BUKA LAGI!` | Montserrat Extra Bold | 56 | #FBB818 with black outline | Center |
| 0:18 - 0:24 | `Bersih Wangi Rapi` | Montserrat Bold | 52 | #FFFFFF with black outline | Bottom center |
| 0:24 - 0:28 | `Tinggal ambil!` | Montserrat Bold | 48 | #FFFFFF with black outline | Bottom center |

**6. Add Transitions**
- Click between each clip on the timeline
- Add **"Cross Dissolve"** transition, duration **0.3 seconds**
- For the transition into the CTA end card, use **"Slide Up"** transition

**7. Add Background Music**
- Click **"Audio"** in the toolbar
- Click **"Music"** then search for these terms:
  - `upbeat corporate`
  - `happy positive`
  - `bright lifestyle`
- Pick a cheerful, upbeat track
- Drag to a second audio track
- **Reduce volume to 15-20%** so the voiceover is clearly dominant
- Fade out music in the last 2 seconds

**8. Enable Auto-Captions**
- Click **"Text"** then **"Auto captions"**
- Select language: **Indonesian (Bahasa Indonesia)**
- Click **"Generate"**
- Review all captions for accuracy
- Style the captions: white text, black background at 60% opacity, Montserrat font

**9. Export**
- Click **"Export"** (top right)
- Resolution: **1080 x 1920**
- Frame rate: **30fps**
- Format: **MP4**
- Quality: **High**
- Click **"Export"**
- Save as: `REEL-01-FINAL.mp4`
