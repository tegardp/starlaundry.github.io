# Operating Model — Multi-Agent Marketing Collaboration

This document defines the practical collaboration model for Star Laundry's multi-agent marketing system. It covers 10 key scenarios that occur in daily/weekly/monthly operations, specifying which agents are involved, in what order, with what inputs and outputs.

## Agent Roster

| Agent | Role |
|-------|------|
| **Marketing Strategist** | Campaign planning, audience strategy, KPI setting |
| **Content Planner** | Content calendar, pillar allocation, idea banking |
| **Short-Form Content** | Reel/TikTok concept development, hooks, format selection |
| **Scriptwriter** | Full script writing (hook → body → CTA) |
| **Copywriter** | Captions, CTAs, WhatsApp copy, ad copy |
| **Visual/Creative** | Visual direction, shot lists, editing guidance |
| **Analytics** | Performance tracking, insights, optimization recommendations |

---

## Scenario 1: Monthly Marketing Planning

**Trigger:** First Monday of each month (or last week of previous month).

**Agents Involved (in order):**
1. **Analytics** — Reviews previous month's performance data
2. **Marketing Strategist** — Sets monthly goals, themes, and priorities based on data
3. **Content Planner** — Translates strategy into a monthly content calendar

**Inputs:**
- Previous month's analytics report (views, engagement, WA inquiries, revenue)
- Active campaigns and their status
- Upcoming holidays/events/seasons
- Budget constraints

**Outputs:**
- Monthly performance summary
- Monthly marketing goals and KPIs
- Monthly content calendar (number of pieces per pillar, per platform)
- Campaign briefs for any new campaigns launching this month

**Time Estimate:** 2-3 hours across all agents

**Example:**
Analytics reports that "Laundry Hack" TikToks got 3x more views than average in February. Marketing Strategist increases educational content allocation from 2x to 3x per week in March. Content Planner adjusts the calendar accordingly and banks 12 new hack ideas.

## Scenario 2: Campaign Launch Planning

**Trigger:** A new campaign is approved (seasonal, launch, or evergreen).

**Agents Involved (in order):**
1. **Marketing Strategist** — Defines campaign objectives, target audience, offers, timeline, and KPIs
2. **Content Planner** — Maps content pieces to the campaign timeline across platforms
3. **Short-Form Content** — Develops reel/TikTok concepts for each planned piece
4. **Copywriter** — Writes WhatsApp broadcast copy and campaign messaging
5. **Visual/Creative** — Sets visual direction and creative guidelines for the campaign

**Inputs:**
- Campaign trigger (holiday, new service, promotion)
- Budget and resource constraints
- Brand guidelines
- Historical performance of similar campaigns

**Outputs:**
- Campaign brief document (see `../campaigns/`)
- Content timeline with assigned dates and platforms
- 8-15 content concepts with hooks
- WhatsApp broadcast messages
- Visual mood board and creative direction

**Time Estimate:** 4-6 hours across all agents (spread over 2-3 days)

**Example:**
Ramadan 2026 campaign. Strategist defines two offers (gordyn diskon 20%, baju Lebaran premium). Content Planner maps 10 pieces across 5 weeks. Short-Form Content develops concepts for each. Copywriter writes 2 WA broadcasts. Visual/Creative sets the warm golden-blue Ramadan aesthetic.

## Scenario 3: Weekly Content Batch Planning

**Trigger:** Every Monday morning.

**Agents Involved (in order):**
1. **Content Planner** — Reviews weekly calendar, assigns content to be produced this week
2. **Short-Form Content** — Selects or develops concepts for each slot
3. **Visual/Creative** — Prepares shot lists for the batch filming session

**Inputs:**
- Monthly content calendar
- Active campaign requirements
- Idea bank (logged ideas)
- Content series schedule (which recurring series are due)
- Trending topics/sounds from the past week

**Outputs:**
- This week's content production list (5-7 pieces)
- Brief for each piece (using `templates/brief-template.md`)
- Shot list for batch filming session
- Filming schedule (day and time)

**Time Estimate:** 1-2 hours

**Example:**
Monday batch: Content Planner assigns 6 pieces for the week — 2 Tumpukan ke Rapi, 1 POV Anak Kos, 1 Berapa KG, 1 Laundry Hack, 1 Promo Flash (Ramadan gordyn). Short-Form Content develops hooks for each. Visual/Creative creates a combined shot list for a Wednesday morning filming session.

## Scenario 4: Creating 10 Reel Ideas from One Campaign Theme

**Trigger:** A campaign is approved and needs content ideas generated.

**Agents Involved (in order):**
1. **Marketing Strategist** — Provides the campaign theme, key messages, and target audience
2. **Content Planner** — Identifies which pillars and series fit the campaign theme
3. **Short-Form Content** — Generates 10 reel concepts, each with a hook, format, and platform recommendation

**Inputs:**
- Campaign brief
- Content pillars list (`content-pillars.md`)
- Content series list (`content-series.md`)
- Platform differences (`platform-differences.md`)

**Outputs:**
- 10 reel ideas, each with:
  - Concept name
  - Pillar
  - Series (if applicable)
  - Hook (1-2 options)
  - Platform recommendation
  - Estimated duration
  - Brief description

**Time Estimate:** 1 hour

**Example:**
Theme: "Musim Hujan." Short-Form Content generates: (1) Musim Hujan vs Star Laundry — bau apek edition, (2) POV anak kos hujan seminggu, (3) Hack: cara baju ga bau apek, (4) Tumpukan ke Rapi — cucian hujan 20 kg, (5) Berapa KG selimut musim hujan, (6) Staff Speed Challenge — lipat handuk basah, (7) Customer Reaction — baju kering dalam 1 jam, (8) Coin Laundry 101 — pakai dryer, (9) Promo Flash — dryer discount, (10) Edukasi — bahaya baju lembab.

## Scenario 5: Turning 1 Content Idea into 3 Hook Variations

**Trigger:** An idea is selected for production and needs hook development.

**Agents Involved (in order):**
1. **Short-Form Content** — Takes the idea and generates 3 distinct hook angles

**Inputs:**
- Content idea (from idea bank or Scenario 4 output)
- Target platform (IG vs TikTok — hooks differ)
- Target audience segment
- Content pillar

**Outputs:**
- 3 hook variations, each with:
  - Hook text (what appears on screen or is spoken)
  - Hook type (question, shock, relatable, visual, challenge)
  - Recommended platform
  - Why this hook works

**Time Estimate:** 15 minutes

**Example:**
Idea: "Cara hilangkan noda kopi dari kemeja putih"

Hook 1 (Question): "Kemeja kena kopi? Jangan panik, pakai cara ini..." — Best for TikTok (voiceover hook)

Hook 2 (Shock): "JANGAN siram air panas ke noda kopi! Ini yang harusnya kamu lakuin..." — Best for TikTok (contrarian hook)

Hook 3 (Visual): Open on close-up of coffee stain on white shirt, zoom out to show the solution — Best for IG Reels (visual-first hook)

## Scenario 6: Turning 1 Hook into a Full Short-Form Script

**Trigger:** A hook is selected and approved from the 3 variations.

**Agents Involved (in order):**
1. **Scriptwriter** — Writes the complete script with structure: Hook → Body → CTA
2. **Short-Form Content** — Reviews the script for format and platform fit

**Inputs:**
- Approved hook
- Content brief (`templates/brief-template.md`)
- Target duration
- Platform-specific requirements (`platform-differences.md`)
- Key message and CTA

**Outputs:**
- Full script with:
  - Timestamp markers (0:00-0:03 Hook, 0:03-0:20 Body, 0:20-0:25 CTA)
  - Voiceover/dialogue text in Indonesian
  - Text overlay instructions
  - Visual direction for each section
  - Audio notes (music, sound effects)

**Time Estimate:** 20-30 minutes

**Example:**
```
SCRIPT: Noda Kopi — Cara Hilangkan
Duration: 25 seconds
Platform: TikTok (primary), IG Reels (adapted)

[0:00-0:03] HOOK
Voiceover: "JANGAN siram air panas ke noda kopi!"
Text overlay: "JANGAN PAKAI AIR PANAS!"
Visual: Close-up of coffee stain on white shirt

[0:03-0:08] STEP 1
Voiceover: "Pertama, siram air dingin dari belakang kain"
Visual: Demonstrating cold water from behind the fabric

[0:08-0:15] STEP 2
Voiceover: "Kedua, olesin sabun cuci piring, diamkan 5 menit"
Visual: Applying dish soap, time-lapse of 5 minutes

[0:15-0:20] RESULT
Voiceover: "Lihat... bersih!"
Visual: Before/after comparison, stain gone
Text overlay: "BERSIH!"

[0:20-0:25] CTA
Voiceover: "Follow Star Laundry buat tips lainnya!"
Text overlay: "Follow + Star Laundry Boyolali"
Visual: End card with logo and WA number
```

## Scenario 8: Adapting One Core Idea for Instagram Reels and TikTok

**Trigger:** Core video is filmed and ready for platform-specific editing.

**Agents Involved (in order):**
1. **Short-Form Content** — Specifies adaptation requirements per `platform-differences.md`
2. **Visual/Creative** — Edits two versions per `../assets/editing-guidelines.md`
3. **Copywriter** — Writes platform-specific captions (Scenario 7)

**Inputs:**
- Raw footage from filming session
- Approved script
- Platform differences checklist
- Editing guidelines

**Outputs:**
- Instagram Reels version (polished edit, custom cover image, music-forward)
- TikTok version (faster cuts, text hooks, trending sound or original audio)
- Platform-specific captions and hashtags
- Scheduled publish times for each platform

**Time Estimate:** 45 minutes (editing two versions from one set of footage)

**Example:**
Core idea: "Tumpukan ke Rapi — 15 kg cucian anak kos"

IG Reels version: Smooth transitions, trending IG music beat-synced, satisfying reveal at the end, custom cover image with text "15 KG → RAPI", 20 sec, caption 150 words with 18 hashtags, CTA "Link WA di bio!"

TikTok version: Jump cuts, speed ramps on machine spinning, text hook "POV: kamu akhirnya nyuci setelah 2 minggu", 18 sec, caption "Anak kos relate? #LaundryBoyolali #FYP", CTA pinned comment with WA number

## Scenario 9: Reviewing Weak-Performing Content and Improving It

**Trigger:** Weekly analytics review identifies underperforming content (below average views/engagement).

**Agents Involved (in order):**
1. **Analytics** — Identifies weak content and diagnoses potential reasons
2. **Short-Form Content** — Proposes improvements or "remix" versions
3. **Scriptwriter** — Rewrites the hook or restructures the script
4. **Visual/Creative** — Re-edits if the issue was visual/pacing

**Inputs:**
- Performance data (views, watch-through rate, engagement rate, saves, shares)
- The original content piece (video + caption)
- Benchmark metrics (average performance for this pillar/series)
- Platform-specific analytics

**Outputs:**
- Diagnosis report: why it underperformed (weak hook? wrong timing? wrong platform? poor pacing?)
- Improvement recommendation (one of):
  - Re-edit with new hook (cheapest fix)
  - Reshoot with new angle
  - Retire the concept
  - Repost at a different time
- Improved version (if re-editing)

**Time Estimate:** 30-45 minutes

**Example:**
Analytics flags: "Laundry Hack: cara cuci sepatu" got 200 views (average is 800). Diagnosis: hook was too generic ("Tips cuci sepatu"), posted at 14:00 WIB (low engagement time), 45 sec duration (too long for the topic). Fix: re-edit with new hook "JANGAN cuci sepatu di mesin cuci biasa!", trim to 25 sec, repost at 12:00 WIB. Result: 1,200 views.

## Scenario 10: Building Repeatable Content Series for Ongoing Publishing

**Trigger:** Content Planner identifies a concept that can become a recurring series, OR a one-off piece performs exceptionally well and should be serialized.

**Agents Involved (in order):**
1. **Content Planner** — Defines the series concept, frequency, and slot in the calendar
2. **Short-Form Content** — Creates the series template (recurring structure, hook formula, shot sequence)
3. **Scriptwriter** — Writes a template script with variable sections
4. **Visual/Creative** — Creates a visual template (consistent intro, branding, end card)

**Inputs:**
- Successful one-off content (or a strong concept idea)
- Content pillars and calendar availability
- Platform performance data (which platform rewards this format)

**Outputs:**
- Series definition (added to `content-series.md`):
  - Series name
  - Format and structure
  - Platform and frequency
  - Template script
  - First 5 episode ideas
- Visual template in CapCut (saved as a project template)
- Calendar slot assignment

**Time Estimate:** 1-2 hours for initial setup, then 15-20 minutes per episode

**Example:**
A one-off "Berapa KG?" video gets 2,500 views (3x average). Content Planner proposes making it a weekly series. Short-Form Content defines the format: show pile, audience guesses, weigh, reveal. Scriptwriter creates a template script with [ITEM TYPE] and [WEIGHT] as variables. Visual/Creative saves a CapCut template with the consistent "TEBAK BERAPA KG?" intro animation. Series launches every Wednesday, becomes a top performer.
