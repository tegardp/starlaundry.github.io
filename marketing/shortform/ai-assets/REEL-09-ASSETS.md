## A. Scene-by-Scene Storyboard

### Scene 1: HOOK (0-5s)
- **Duration:** 5 seconds
- **Visual:** Dramatic scene — alarm clock ringing, calendar showing "BESOK" circled in red, wrinkled clothes in background. Panicked energy with fast zoom
- **Text Overlay:** "Besok ada acara, baju belum dicuci?"
- **Voiceover:** "Besok ada acara, baju belum dicuci? Jangan panik!"
- **Transition:** Smash cut (hard cut)

### Scene 2: THE PROBLEM (5-10s)
- **Duration:** 5 seconds
- **Visual:** Close-up of wrinkled, dirty clothes piled up. Red warning/alert visual elements. Timer counting down with urgency. Stressed mood lighting
- **Text Overlay:** "Cucian numpuk, waktu mepet..."
- **Voiceover:** "Cucian numpuk, waktu mepet. Tenang..."
- **Transition:** Quick swipe up

### Scene 3: THE SOLUTION — EXPRESS 6 JAM (10-18s)
- **Duration:** 8 seconds
- **Visual:** Bright transformation — dirty clothes on left, arrow with spinning clock in middle, fresh clean folded clothes on right. Big "6 JAM" text with timer graphic. Price tag showing "Rp 13.000/kg". Blue and yellow color scheme, clean and confident
- **Text Overlay:** "Cuci Setrika Wangi - 6 JAM - Rp 13.000/kg"
- **Voiceover:** "Star Laundry punya layanan express! Cuci Setrika Wangi, cuma enam jam. Tiga belas ribu per kilo."
- **Transition:** Slide up

### Scene 4: EVEN FASTER OPTION (18-23s)
- **Duration:** 5 seconds
- **Visual:** Speed lines background. "3 JAM" text even bigger and bolder with lightning bolt icons. Price tag "Rp 8.000/kg". Folded clean clothes icon. Yellow (#FBB818) background with blue accents for energy
- **Text Overlay:** "Cuci Lipat Rapi - 3 JAM - Rp 8.000/kg"
- **Voiceover:** "Atau lebih cepat lagi! Cuci Lipat Rapi, tiga jam aja. Delapan ribu per kilo."
- **Transition:** Pop zoom

### Scene 5: TIMELINE VISUAL (23-27s)
- **Duration:** 4 seconds
- **Visual:** Simple timeline graphic — clock showing 3:00 PM "Drop off" on left, arrow, clock showing 9:00 PM "Ambil!" on right. Checkmark icon. Clean infographic style
- **Text Overlay:** "Drop off jam 3 siang, jam 9 malam udah beres!"
- **Voiceover:** "Drop off sebelum jam tiga siang, jam sembilan malam udah bisa diambil!"
- **Transition:** Zoom out

### Scene 6: CTA (27-30s)
- **Duration:** 3 seconds
- **Visual:** Star Laundry logo, WhatsApp icon with number, "Jangan panik, Star Laundry solusinya!" tagline. Blue gradient background
- **Text Overlay:** "WA sekarang: 0822-2567-2756"
- **Voiceover:** "Hubungi Star Laundry sekarang!"
- **Transition:** Fade out

## F. CapCut Assembly Instructions

### Step 1: Project Setup
1. Open CapCut, create new project, set to 9:16 (1080x1920)
2. Set project duration to 30 seconds

### Step 2: Import Assets
1. Import all 6 images from Section B
2. Import 3 video clips from Section C
3. Import voiceover audio file
4. Search and add background music from CapCut library using terms in Section E

### Step 3: Timeline Assembly
1. **0-5s (HOOK):** Place Video Scene 1 (alarm clock ringing) OR Image 1. Add fast zoom-in keyframe animation. Add text "Besok ada acara, baju belum dicuci?" in bold white with red accent. Use "Shake" text animation
2. **5-10s (PROBLEM):** Place Image 2 (dirty clothes pile). Add slow zoom and slight red color filter/overlay for tension. Text "Cucian numpuk, waktu mepet..." in white
3. **10-18s (6 JAM SOLUTION):** Place Image 3 (before/after split) as base. Overlay Video Scene 2 (clock fast forward) in center. Add price text "Cuci Setrika Wangi - 6 JAM - Rp 13.000/kg" in yellow (#FBB818) bold. Use "Pop" animation for price appearing
4. **18-23s (3 JAM OPTION):** Place Image 4 (3 hour speed graphic). Add pulsing/scaling keyframe animation for the "3 JAM" text. Lightning bolt stickers from CapCut library. Price text in white bold
5. **23-27s (TIMELINE):** Place Image 5 (timeline infographic). Animate the arrow drawing from left clock to right clock using CapCut keyframes. Add checkmark pop at the end
6. **27-30s (CTA):** Place Image 6 (end screen) OR Video Scene 3 (fresh clothes reveal) with logo overlay. Static, clean

### Step 4: Text Overlays
1. **Font:** Bold sans-serif (Montserrat Bold or Impact for urgency)
2. **Hook text (0-5s):** White text, large, with red "belum dicuci?" highlight
3. **Price text:** Yellow (#FBB818) with white outline for readability
4. **Time text (6 JAM, 3 JAM):** Extra large, blue (#2AADE0), bold with glow effect
5. **CTA text:** White on blue background
6. Position all text in center-to-lower-third safe zone

### Step 5: Transitions & Effects
1. Scene 1 to 2: Hard smash cut (no transition, instant cut for impact)
2. Scene 2 to 3: Quick "Swipe Up" (0.2s) — mood shift from problem to solution
3. Scene 3 to 4: "Slide Up" (0.3s)
4. Scene 4 to 5: "Pop" zoom (0.2s)
5. Scene 5 to 6: "Zoom Out" (0.3s)

### Step 6: Speed & Urgency Effects
1. Add a subtle screen shake effect on Scene 1 (CapCut: Effects > Video Effects > Shake)
2. Add speed lines overlay from CapCut stickers on Scene 4
3. Add a brief flash/white frame between Scene 2 and 3 (the moment of "solution reveal")
4. Optional: Add ticking clock sound effect for first 10 seconds (CapCut: Audio > Sound Effects > search "clock tick")

### Step 7: Color Grading
1. Scenes 1-2 (problem): Slightly desaturated, warm/red tint (tension)
2. Scenes 3-6 (solution): Bright, saturated, cool blue tint (confidence)
3. This color shift reinforces the problem-to-solution narrative

### Step 8: Audio
1. Place voiceover on audio track 1
2. Place background music on audio track 2 at 25-30% volume
3. Add clock ticking sound effect on audio track 3 for 0-10s at 15% volume
4. Add a satisfying "ding" sound effect when the 6 JAM and 3 JAM text appears
5. Fade music in (0.3s) and out (1s)

### Step 9: Final Touches
1. Add Star Laundry watermark/logo in top-right corner (small, semi-transparent)
2. Preview full reel and ensure urgency builds then resolves
3. Check that all prices are clearly readable
4. Export at 1080x1920, 30fps, high quality
