**Duration:** 30 seconds | **Type:** Promo/Referral | **Format:** 9:16 Vertical

## A. Scene-by-Scene Storyboard

### Scene 1: HOOK (0-5s)
- **Duration:** 5 seconds
- **Visual:** Split screen — left side shows person with overflowing laundry basket looking stressed, right side shows a phone screen with a chat bubble
- **Text Overlay:** "Punya temen yang cuciannya numpuk?"
- **Voiceover:** "Punya temen yang cuciannya numpuk? Kasih tau dia ini..."
- **Transition:** Quick zoom into phone screen

### Scene 2: THE DEAL (5-12s)
- **Duration:** 7 seconds
- **Visual:** Big bold "Rp 5.000 OFF" graphic in center. Two person icons on left and right connected by a handshake icon. Blue (#2AADE0) background with yellow (#FBB818) discount badge
- **Text Overlay:** "DISKON Rp 5.000 BERDUA! Kamu hemat, temen juga hemat"
- **Voiceover:** "Ajak temen kamu cuci di Star Laundry, kalian berdua dapat diskon lima ribu rupiah!"
- **Transition:** Slide left

### Scene 3: STEP 1 (12-17s)
- **Duration:** 5 seconds
- **Visual:** Three numbered circles (1-2-3) appear sequentially. Circle 1 lights up. Shows chat bubble icon with "Eh, coba Star Laundry deh!"
- **Text Overlay:** "1. Kasih tau temen kamu"
- **Voiceover:** "Caranya gampang. Pertama, kasih tau temen kamu soal Star Laundry."
- **Transition:** Slide left

### Scene 4: STEPS 2-3 (17-23s)
- **Duration:** 6 seconds
- **Visual:** Circle 2 lights up — laundry bag with name tag icon. Circle 3 lights up — two discount tags falling like confetti
- **Text Overlay:** "2. Temen drop off + sebut nomormu / 3. Berdua dapat diskon!"
- **Voiceover:** "Temen kamu tinggal drop off sambil sebut nomor kamu. Selesai! Berdua langsung dapat diskon."
- **Transition:** Pop/bounce

### Scene 5: STAMP CARD REMINDER (23-27s)
- **Duration:** 4 seconds
- **Visual:** Stamp card graphic showing 10 stamps, last one glowing with "GRATIS" text. Yellow background
- **Text Overlay:** "Jangan lupa: 10x cuci = 1x GRATIS"
- **Voiceover:** "Plus, jangan lupa kumpulin stamp. Sepuluh kali cuci, satu kali gratis!"
- **Transition:** Zoom out

### Scene 6: CTA (27-30s)
- **Duration:** 3 seconds
- **Visual:** Star Laundry logo centered. WhatsApp icon with number. "Tag temen kamu!" text with arrow pointing down
- **Text Overlay:** "Tag temen kamu sekarang! WA 0822-2567-2756"
- **Voiceover:** "Tag temen kamu sekarang!"
- **Transition:** Fade out

## F. CapCut Assembly Instructions

### Step 1: Project Setup
1. Open CapCut, create new project, set to 9:16 (1080x1920)
2. Set project duration to 30 seconds

### Step 2: Import Assets
1. Import all AI-generated images (5 images from Section B)
2. Import AI video clips (3 clips from Section C)
3. Import voiceover audio file
4. Search and add background music from CapCut library using search terms in Section E

### Step 3: Timeline Assembly
1. **0-5s:** Place Image 1 (split screen hook) or Video Scene 1 (laundry pile). Add text overlay "Punya temen yang cuciannya numpuk?" in white bold font, center screen
2. **5-12s:** Place Image 2 (discount badge) or Video Scene 2 (badge pop). Add text "DISKON Rp 5.000 BERDUA!" in large yellow text
3. **12-17s:** Place Image 3 (three steps). Animate Step 1 circle to appear with pop effect
4. **17-23s:** Continue Image 3 with Steps 2-3 animating in. Use CapCut text animation for each step appearing one by one
5. **23-27s:** Place Image 4 (stamp card). Add subtle zoom-in animation
6. **27-30s:** Place Image 5 (CTA end screen). Static, clean

### Step 4: Text Overlays
1. All text: Use bold sans-serif font (Montserrat or Poppins)
2. Primary text color: White with dark drop shadow
3. Accent text (prices, discounts): Yellow (#FBB818)
4. Position all text in the center-to-lower-third safe zone
5. Add text appear animations: "Pop" or "Typewriter"

### Step 5: Transitions
1. Scene 1 to 2: "Zoom" transition (0.3s)
2. Scene 2 to 3: "Slide Left" transition (0.3s)
3. Scene 3 to 4: "Slide Left" transition (0.3s)
4. Scene 4 to 5: "Pop" transition (0.2s)
5. Scene 5 to 6: "Zoom Out" transition (0.3s)

### Step 6: Audio
1. Place voiceover on audio track 1, align with scenes
2. Place background music on audio track 2
3. Set music volume to 20-25%
4. Add slight fade-in (0.5s) at start and fade-out (1s) at end for music

### Step 7: Final Touches
1. Add Star Laundry watermark/logo in top-right corner (small, semi-transparent) throughout
2. Preview full reel, check text readability
3. Export at 1080x1920, 30fps, high quality
