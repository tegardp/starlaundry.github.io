## A. Scene-by-Scene Storyboard

### Scene 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:05)
- **Duration**: 5 seconds
- **Visual**: AI-generated image of an open suitcase overflowing with wrinkled, colorful clothes on a bedroom floor. Messy room background.

### Scene 2 — PROBLEM (0:05–0:12)

- **Text overlay**: "TUMPUKAN BAJU KOTOR" (big bold yellow #FBB818 text, slight shake animation)
- **Voiceover line**: "Baju kotor numpuk dari perjalanan... males banget nyuci sendiri."

### Scene 3 — SOLUTION (0:12–0:20)
- **Duration**: 8 seconds
- **Visual**: AI-generated image of a bright, modern laundromat interior with blue-themed washing machines running (use Kling video for spinning motion). Clean, well-lit space.
- **Text overlay**: "Serahin ke STAR LAUNDRY!" (white text with blue #2AADE0 highlight box behind "STAR LAUNDRY")
- **Voiceover line**: "Langsung aja bawa ke Star Laundry Boyolali! Tinggal drop off, beres."

### Scene 4 — TRANSFORMATION (0:20–0:26)
- **Duration**: 6 seconds

- **Text overlay**: "BERSIH, WANGI, RAPI!" (big bold text, each word pops in sequentially, yellow #FBB818)
- **Voiceover line**: "Bersih, wangi, rapi... tinggal pakai lagi!"
- **Transition**: Scale down into corner, revealing Scene 5 CTA

### Scene 5 — CTA (0:26–0:30)
- **Duration**: 4 seconds
- **Visual**: Solid blue #2AADE0 background with Star Laundry logo (or text "STAR LAUNDRY" in bold). Yellow accent elements.

## F. CapCut Assembly Instructions

### Step 1: Import Assets
1. Open CapCut, create new project (9:16 vertical)
2. Import all 5 AI-generated images (or 4 images + 1 Kling video for Scene 3)
3. Import the voiceover audio file (exported from ElevenLabs/CapCut TTS)

### Step 2: Timeline Arrangement
1. Place Scene 1 image: 0:00–0:05 (apply slow Ken Burns zoom-in effect)
2. Place Scene 2 image: 0:05–0:12 (apply slow zoom-out or slight pan-down)
3. Place Scene 3 image/video: 0:12–0:20 (apply slow zoom-in if image; if Kling video, trim to fit)
4. Place Scene 4 image: 0:20–0:26 (apply slow Ken Burns zoom-in)
5. Place Scene 5 CTA image: 0:26–0:30 (static, no movement)

### Step 3: Transitions
1. Between Scene 1 and 2: "Zoom" transition (0.3s)
2. Between Scene 2 and 3: "Slide Left" transition (0.3s)
3. Between Scene 3 and 4: "Dissolve" transition (0.5s)
4. Between Scene 4 and 5: "Scale" transition (0.3s)

### Step 4: Text Overlay Placement
1. Scene 1 (0:00–0:05): Add text "Baru pulang mudik?" — font: bold sans-serif, size: large, color: white, position: center. At 0:02 add second text "Isi koper pasti kayak gini..." below it.
2. Scene 2 (0:05–0:12): Add text "TUMPUKAN BAJU KOTOR" — font: extra bold, size: extra large, color: yellow #FBB818, position: center. Add "shake" animation.
3. Scene 3 (0:12–0:20): Add text "Serahin ke" (white, medium) above "STAR LAUNDRY!" (white text inside blue #2AADE0 box, large). Use "pop" entrance animation.
4. Scene 4 (0:20–0:26): Add three texts that appear sequentially (0.5s apart): "BERSIH" then "WANGI" then "RAPI!" — all yellow #FBB818, extra bold, center. Use "typewriter" or "pop" animation.
5. Scene 5 (0:26–0:30): Add text "WA 0822-2567-2756" (white, large, center) and "Buka 07.00–21.00" (yellow, medium, below).

### Step 5: Audio Layering
1. Place voiceover on audio track 1, align with scenes
2. Add background music on audio track 2
3. Set music volume to 15–20% during voiceover, 50% during non-voiceover moments
4. Add a slight fade-in on music at 0:00 (0.5s) and fade-out at 0:29 (1s)

### Step 6: Captions
1. Use CapCut auto-caption feature (Bahasa Indonesia)
2. Style: white text, black outline, bottom-center position
3. Review and correct any TTS transcription errors

### Step 7: Export Settings
1. Resolution: 1080 x 1920
2. Frame rate: 30 fps
3. Quality: High
4. Format: MP4
5. Export and upload to Instagram Reels
