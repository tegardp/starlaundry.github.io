## How To Use In CapCut

### Shop Interior (shop-interior.jpg)
- **As background**: Import → place on timeline → apply slow Ken Burns zoom-in (3-5s)
- **As reveal shot**: Start blurred → transition to sharp (for "solution" beats)
- **As split screen**: Crop left half (machines) or right half (counter) for variety
- **Tip**: Apply slight blue color grade to match brand color #2AADE0

### Logo (logo.png)
- **End card**: Place on white/blue background for last 2-3 seconds of every reel
- **Watermark**: Small, semi-transparent in corner throughout the video
- **Intro flash**: Quick 0.5s logo pop at the start before hook

### Pricelist (pricelist-kiloan.jpeg)
- **Full screen**: Show for 2-3 seconds when mentioning prices
- **Zoomed crop**: Zoom into specific service row (e.g., Self-Service Rp 10,000 row for Reel #5)
- **Background**: Use behind text overlays with 50% opacity
