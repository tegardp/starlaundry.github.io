## DETAILED FINDINGS

### ISSUE 1: Bonus Stamp Referral Claim (NEEDS BUSINESS CONFIRMATION)

**Severity:** Medium
**Files affected:**
- SF-20260407-07.md (Brief, line 53)
- SCRIPT-20260407-07.md (Script, lines 63-68)
- CAPTION-20260407-07.md (Caption, IG + TikTok)
- WA-WEEK3-POST-LEBARAN.md (WA Broadcast 5, line 30)

**Issue:** All referral content states: "Kalau temen kamu pakai layanan dalam 7 hari, kamu dapet 2 stamp ekstra." This promotion is NOT in the approved pricing reference. The reference only lists: "Stamp Card: 10 washes = 1 free."

**Risk:** If this bonus stamp incentive has not been approved by operations, it could create customer expectations that cannot be fulfilled.

**Action required:** Business owner must confirm whether the "2 bonus stamps for referral within 7 days" promotion is approved. If not approved, remove from all 4 files.

---

### ISSUE 2: Unverified Promo Names in WA Broadcast 6

**Severity:** Medium
**File:** WA-WEEK3-POST-LEBARAN.md (WA Broadcast 6, lines 63-66)

**Issue:** The thank-you broadcast lists four promos:
- Diskon Cuci Pertama
- Senin Hemat
- Stamp Card
- Referral Ajak Teman

"Diskon Cuci Pertama" and "Senin Hemat" are NOT in the approved pricing reference. Only Stamp Card (10 washes = 1 free) and Referral (Rp 5,000 off both parties) are documented.

**Risk:** Customers may ask about "Diskon Cuci Pertama" or "Senin Hemat" and staff won't know what to offer.

**Action required:** Business owner must confirm these promos exist. If not, remove from the broadcast.
