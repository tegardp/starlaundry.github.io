# Repeatable Content Series — Star Laundry Boyolali

These are recurring content series that Star Laundry publishes on a regular schedule. Each series has a defined format, structure, and episode pipeline so the team can batch-produce content efficiently.

---

## 1. Tumpukan ke Rapi

**Format:** Transformation / Satisfying
**Recurring Structure:** Start with a messy pile of laundry (the bigger/messier the better), then show the full process at Star Laundry, end with the neat, folded, packaged result.
**Platform:** Instagram Reels (primary), TikTok (adapted)
**Frequency:** 2x per week
**Duration:** 15-30 seconds

**Template Script Structure:**
```
[Shot 1] Close-up of messy/dirty laundry pile — 2 sec
[Shot 2] Weighing the pile — "X kg nih!" — 2 sec
[Shot 3] Loading into machine — 2 sec
[Shot 4] Machine spinning (speed-ramped) — 3 sec
[Shot 5] Fresh out of dryer — 2 sec
[Shot 6] Folding sequence (satisfying) — 3 sec
[Shot 7] Final packed result — 3 sec
[End card] Star Laundry logo + WA CTA — 2 sec
Music: upbeat trending track, beat-synced cuts
```

**Episode Ideas:**
1. "12 kg cucian anak kos — dari chaos ke rapi"
2. "Sprei hotel numpuk 30 kg — beres dalam sehari"
3. "Baju kerja seminggu — tumpukan kantor ke rapi"
4. "Gordyn kotor berbulan-bulan — transformasi satisfying"
5. "Cucian keluarga weekend — 20 kg jadi rapi semua"

## Batch Production Tips

1. **Film 5-7 episodes in one session** — dedicate 2 hours on a quiet morning
2. **Keep a "pile bank"** — save interesting laundry piles for Tumpukan ke Rapi and Berapa KG
3. **Brief staff in advance** — they need to know their role for Speed Challenge and BTS
4. **Capture customer reactions opportunistically** — always have phone ready
5. **Edit in batches** — edit all footage from one session together in CapCut
6. **Schedule ahead** — use Meta Business Suite (IG) and TikTok scheduler
