# Customer Funnel Map — Star Laundry Boyolali

```
AWARENESS → INTEREST → TRIAL → EXPERIENCE → REPEAT → ADVOCATE
```

---

## Stage 1: AWARENESS — "Orang Tau Star Laundry Ada"

**Definition**: Target audience learns that Star Laundry exists in Boyolali.

**Channels**:
| Channel | Tactic | Priority |
|---------|--------|----------|
| Google Maps | Optimized profile, photos, keywords | HIGH |
| TikTok | Viral/educational content | HIGH |
| Instagram | Brand awareness posts | HIGH |
| Spanduk | 5–8 strategic locations radius 2km | HIGH |
| Flyers | Door-to-door at kos-kosan | MEDIUM |
| Partnerships | Brosur at kos, warung, toko baju | MEDIUM |

**Content Types**: Satisfying/transformation videos, POV relatable, edukasi tips, outlet tour

**Offers at This Stage**: None — just awareness. Show quality, pricing, and convenience.

**KPIs**:
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Google Maps impressions | Track monthly |
| TikTok video views | >1,000/video average |
| IG reach/week | >500 |
| Spanduk locations | 5–8 active |

**Conversion to Next Stage**: Person looks up Star Laundry on Google Maps, visits profile, or watches multiple videos.

---

## Stage 2: INTEREST — "Orang Tertarik Coba"

**Definition**: Person is interested and evaluating whether to try Star Laundry.

**Channels**:
| Channel | Tactic | Priority |
|---------|--------|----------|
| Google Maps | Reviews (4.5+ rating), photos of clean outlet | HIGH |
| Instagram | Pricing info, social proof, process videos | HIGH |
| Website | Price page, service details, FAQ | MEDIUM |
| Word of mouth | Friends/neighbors recommend | HIGH |

**Content Types**: Social proof (reviews, testimonials), pricing posts, FAQ answers, comparison content (vs traditional laundry)

**Offers at This Stage**: Highlight always-on promos — Cuci pertama Rp 3,000/kg, Self-service Rp 10,000/7kg

**Common Questions (address in content)**:
- "Berapa harganya?" → Show clear pricing
- "Di mana?" → Show Google Maps link
- "Bisa antar jemput?" → Yes, gratis radius 3km
- "Aman nggak? Nggak hilang?" → Show labeling system + guarantee

**KPIs**:
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Google Maps rating | 4.5+ |
| Google Maps reviews | 100 in 3 months |
| Website visits/month | Track |
| IG profile visits/week | >100 |
| "Tau dari mana?" responses | Track sources |

**Conversion to Next Stage**: Person contacts via WhatsApp, visits outlet, or tries self-service.

---

## Stage 3: TRIAL — "Coba Pertama Kali"

**Definition**: First-time customer tries Star Laundry.

**Channels**:
| Channel | Tactic | Priority |
|---------|--------|----------|
| WhatsApp | Respond fast, be friendly, guide ordering | HIGH |
| In-outlet | Clean, bright, welcoming. Staff greets by name if possible. | HIGH |
| Walk-in | Standing banner, clear pricing display | MEDIUM |

**Offers at This Stage**:
- **Cuci pertama Rp 3,000/kg** — reduce barrier to try
- **Self-service Rp 10,000/7kg** — low commitment entry
- **Referral invite** — if referred by friend, both get Rp 5,000 off

**Trial Experience Checklist**:
- [ ] Greeting: friendly, call them "Kak"
- [ ] Explain service options clearly
- [ ] Capture WA number (for notifications + future broadcasts)
- [ ] Give stamp card
- [ ] Set realistic timeline ("besok sore ya, Kak")
- [ ] Give review card (QR to Google Maps)

**KPIs**:
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| New customers/week | Min 10 |
| First-timer conversion to order | >70% of inquiries |
| WA number captured | 100% of transactions |

**Conversion to Next Stage**: Customer receives clean, on-time, quality results.

---

## Stage 4: EXPERIENCE — "Pengalaman Positif"

**Definition**: Customer receives results and forms an opinion.

**Critical Factors** (this makes or breaks repeat):
| Factor | Standard |
|--------|----------|
| Cleanliness | Bersih, tidak ada noda tersisa |
| Scent | Wangi segar |
| Folding | Rapi, seragam |
| Timing | Tepat waktu sesuai janji |
| Communication | WA notification saat selesai |
| Accuracy | Tidak kurang, tidak tertukar |

**Offers at This Stage**: None needed — quality IS the offer.

**Touchpoints**:
1. WA notification: "Cucian Kak [Nama] sudah selesai ya! Bisa diambil di outlet atau mau kami antar? 😊"
2. At pickup: "Makasih ya Kak! Jangan lupa stamp card-nya ya"
3. Review card: slip in with finished laundry

**Failure Recovery**:
- If complaint: "Mohon maaf Kak, kami cuci ulang GRATIS"
- If late: proactive WA before deadline explaining delay
- If item issue: escalate immediately, solve same day if possible

**KPIs**:
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Complaint rate | <5% of orders |
| On-time rate | >90% |
| Satisfaction (informal) | Positive WA replies |

**Conversion to Next Stage**: Customer comes back for second order.

---

## Stage 5: REPEAT — "Jadi Langganan"

**Definition**: Customer uses Star Laundry regularly (2+ times/month).

**Channels**:
| Channel | Tactic | Priority |
|---------|--------|----------|
| WhatsApp | Broadcast promos 2x/week | HIGH |
| Stamp card | 10x cuci = 1x gratis | HIGH |
| In-outlet | Remember names, personal touch | MEDIUM |

**Offers at This Stage**:
- **Stamp card**: cuci 10x = gratis 1x cuci lipat
- **Paket bulanan**: 20kg/Rp 90,000 or 40kg/Rp 160,000
- **Senin Hemat**: Rp 4,000/kg every Monday
- **WA exclusive promos**: flash sales for broadcast recipients

**Retention Tactics**:
- Call by name (check WA data)
- Birthday WA greeting + discount (if birth date known)
- "Kangen cucian Star Laundry Kak? 😄" re-engagement for inactive
- Stamp card progress reminders: "Sudah cuci 8x, tinggal 2x lagi GRATIS!"

**KPIs**:
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Repeat customer rate | 60%+ |
| Average orders/customer/month | 2+ |
| Stamp card redemption rate | Track |
| Paket bulanan subscribers | Track monthly |
| WA broadcast read rate | >70% |

**Conversion to Next Stage**: Customer recommends Star Laundry to others.

---

## Stage 6: ADVOCATE — "Rekomendasikan ke Orang Lain"

**Definition**: Loyal customer actively promotes Star Laundry to friends, family, neighbors.

**Channels**:
| Channel | Tactic | Priority |
|---------|--------|----------|
| Referral program | Rp 5,000 off for both (referrer + referee) | HIGH |
| Google Maps review | Rp 2,000 discount for review | HIGH |
| Word of mouth | Natural recommendation | HIGH |
| Social media | Customer tagging/sharing | MEDIUM |

**Offers at This Stage**:
- **Referral**: "Ajak teman, keduanya dapat diskon Rp 5,000"
- **Review incentive**: "Review di Google Maps, dapat diskon Rp 2,000"
- **Social share**: "Post story @starlaundry.boyolali, dapat diskon Rp 1,000"

**Advocate Activation Tactics**:
- Staff asks satisfied customers: "Kalau puas, boleh rekomendasikan ke teman ya, Kak! Ada diskon 5,000 buat Kakak dan temannya"
- Follow-up WA: review request 1 day after pickup
- Share-worthy moments: make packaging/folding "Instagram-worthy"

**KPIs**:
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Google Maps reviews/month | +20 |
| Referral redemptions/month | Track |
| Customer referral rate | Track "tau dari mana: teman" |
| Social mentions | Track tags/mentions |

## Funnel Health Indicators

| Signal | Meaning | Action |
|--------|---------|--------|
| High awareness, low trial | Interest stage is weak — content not convincing | More social proof, review pushes |
| High trial, low repeat | Experience stage failing — quality issues | Fix SOP, address complaints |
| High repeat, low advocacy | Customers satisfied but not spreading | Activate referral program, ask for reviews |
| Low awareness overall | Not enough visibility | Increase offline (spanduk) + social posting |
