3. Content: 3 viral-potential TikToks (>10K views)

- Duration: April 1–18 (pre-Lebaran)
- Offer: Cuci gordyn/sprei diskon 20%, Cuci setrika baju Lebaran Rp 8,000/kg
- Content: 10 pieces (4 edukasi, 3 satisfying, 2 promo, 1 social proof)
- WA broadcasts: 4 special Lebaran broadcasts
- Budget: Rp 300K (flyer Lebaran + kartu promo)
