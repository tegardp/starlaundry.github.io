## Pillar 1: Edukasi Laundry (25%)

**Description**: Practical tips, myth-busting, how-to content about laundry and fabric care. Positions Star Laundry as the knowledgeable expert.

**Formats**: Talking head + B-roll, text overlay tutorials, comparison demos
**Platforms**: TikTok (primary), IG Reels
**Best Length**: 30–60 seconds

**10 Content Ideas with Hooks**:
1. "Kenapa baju kamu bau padahal sudah dicuci?" — myth-busting
2. "3 kesalahan cuci baju yang sering dilakukan" — listicle
3. "Jangan cuci baju pakai air panas kalau..." — conditional warning
4. "Berapa kali sebaiknya cuci jeans?" — question hook
5. "Cara menghilangkan noda kopi di baju" — how-to
6. "Kenapa jemur matahari bikin baju cepat rusak?" — counter-intuitive
7. "Suhu air yang tepat untuk berbagai jenis kain" — expert tip
8. "Tanda-tanda detergent yang tidak cocok untuk baju kamu" — diagnostic
9. "Cara menyimpan baju biar tetap wangi" — practical tip
10. "Ini yang terjadi kalau mesin cuci overload" — warning/demo

**Success Metrics**: Views, watch completion rate, saves, shares

## Pillar 2: Satisfying / Transformation (20%)

**Description**: Visually satisfying content showing laundry processes and dramatic before/after transformations. High viral potential.

**Formats**: Speed-up process shots, before/after transitions, close-up ASMR-style
**Platforms**: Both (strongest viral potential)
**Best Length**: 15–30 seconds

**10 Content Ideas with Hooks**:
1. "Tumpukan cucian ini jadi segini rapi" — transformation reveal
2. Lipatan baju speed up dari atas — satisfying overhead angle
3. Setrika uap close-up — wrinkles disappearing in real time
4. Baju keluar dari dryer — warm steam visual
5. Before/after baju kotor banget → bersih — split screen
6. Deretan cucian rapi di rak — aesthetic shot
7. Time lapse: 50kg cucian dari masuk mesin sampai terlipat
8. Mesin cuci 20kg drum spinning — hypnotic angle
9. Color-sorted folding — all whites, all colors, all darks
10. Packing cucian untuk delivery — neat and wrapped

**Success Metrics**: Views (viral indicator), shares, follows from non-followers

## Pillar 7: Interaktif / Challenge (5%)

**Description**: Interactive content that encourages participation, comments, and engagement.

**Formats**: Polls, guessing games, challenges, Q&A
**Platforms**: TikTok (challenges), IG Stories (polls/Q&A)
**Best Length**: 15–30 seconds

**10 Content Ideas with Hooks**:
1. "Tebak berapa KG cucian ini?" — guessing game
2. Poll: "Lebih suka cuci sendiri atau full service?"
3. "Caption this" — foto tumpukan cucian
4. Q&A: "Tanya apa aja tentang laundry"
5. "This or that: setrika sendiri vs setrika uap?"
6. Speed folding challenge — staff competition
7. "Tebak harganya!" — show pile, guess total cost
8. "Mana yang lebih kotor?" — show two items, vote
9. "Kirim foto cucian numpuk kamu!" — UGC prompt
10. "Challenge: lipat kaos dalam 5 detik ⏱"

**Success Metrics**: Comments (engagement), poll responses, UGC submissions
