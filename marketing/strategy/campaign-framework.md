# Campaign Planning Framework

## Campaign Types

### 1. Launch Campaigns
One-time campaigns for new services, new outlets, or major business events.
- Lead time: 4+ weeks
- Duration: 2–4 weeks
- Example: "Grand opening", "Layanan baru: Paket Bulanan"

### 2. Evergreen Campaigns
Always-on campaigns that run continuously.
- Lead time: Initial setup only
- Duration: Ongoing
- Examples: Senin Hemat, Promo Pelajar, Cuci Pertama, Stamp Card, Referral

### 3. Seasonal Campaigns
Time-bound campaigns tied to calendar events.
- Lead time: 4 weeks
- Duration: 2–6 weeks
- Examples: Ramadan/Lebaran, Musim Hujan, Back to School, 17 Agustus

## Example Campaigns

### Campaign 1: "Bersih Sambut Lebaran" (Seasonal)

**Dates**: March 15 — April 18, 2026 (4 weeks pre-Lebaran)

**Objective**:
- Primary: +30% revenue from sprei/gordyn/baju Lebaran services
- Secondary: +25 new customers trying Star Laundry for Lebaran prep

**Target**: Keluarga Muda (primary), Pekerja Kantoran (secondary)

**Offer**:
- Cuci gordyn & sprei diskon 20%
- Cuci setrika baju Lebaran Rp 8,000/kg (premium finish)
- First-timer Ramadan bonus: Rp 2,500/kg (instead of Rp 3,000)

**Content Plan (10 pieces)**:
| # | Pillar | Concept | Platform |
|---|--------|---------|----------|
| 1 | Edukasi | "Cara cuci gordyn yang benar sebelum Lebaran" | TikTok |
| 2 | Satisfying | Gordyn kotor → bersih transformation | Both |
| 3 | Promo | Lebaran promo announcement | IG + WA |
| 4 | Edukasi | "Sprei harus dicuci berapa kali sebulan?" | TikTok |
| 5 | POV | "POV: bersih-bersih rumah jelang Lebaran" | TikTok |
| 6 | Social Proof | Customer reaction: clean sprei/gordyn | IG |
| 7 | Edukasi | "Tips merawat baju Lebaran biar awet" | Both |
| 8 | Satisfying | Baju Lebaran disetrika uap — close up | Both |
| 9 | Promo | "Last call" — deadline reminder | IG + WA |
| 10 | Behind Scene | "Begini sibuknya laundry jelang Lebaran" | TikTok |

**WA Broadcasts (4)**:
1. Week 1: Promo announcement
2. Week 2: Reminder + tips cuci gordyn
3. Week 3: Urgency — "tinggal 1 minggu sebelum Lebaran"
4. Week 4: Last call + Lebaran greeting

### Campaign 2: "Promo Pelajar" (Evergreen)

**Dates**: Always on

**Objective**: Capture anak kos segment, build habit

**Target**: Anak Kos / Mahasiswa

**Offer**: 15% diskon dengan KTM/kartu pelajar

**Content Plan (refresh quarterly)**:
- 2 POV "anak kos" TikToks per month
- 1 promo reminder per month on IG
- WA broadcast at start of semester

| 7 | Promo rainy day discount announcement | IG + WA |
| 8 | "Star Laundry vs cuaca" compilation | TikTok |
