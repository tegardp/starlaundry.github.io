# Channel Strategy — Star Laundry Boyolali

## Channel Overview

| Channel | Role in Funnel | Primary Segment | Frequency | KPI |
|---------|---------------|-----------------|-----------|-----|
| WhatsApp | Conversion + Retention | All (existing customers) | 2x/week | Response rate, orders |
| Instagram | Awareness + Interest | All | 3x/week | Reach, engagement, profile visits |
| TikTok | Awareness (top of funnel) | Anak Kos, broad reach | 3x/week | Views, followers, virality |
| Google Maps | Interest + Trust | New customers searching | 1x/week | Reviews, rating, impressions |
| Offline | Awareness (local) | All local residents | Ongoing | Walk-ins, flyer redemption |

---

## WhatsApp — Primary Conversion Channel

**Role**: Convert interest into transactions. Retain existing customers. Direct communication line.

**Content Types**:
- Monday promo broadcast (Senin Hemat)
- Friday weekend reminder broadcast
- Seasonal promo announcements
- Order confirmations + pickup notifications (automated)
- Personal follow-ups for VIP customers

**Frequency**: 2 broadcasts/week maximum. More = spam = unsubscribe.

**KPIs**:
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Database size | +20 contacts/week |
| Broadcast read rate | >70% |
| Reply/response rate | >10% |
| Orders from broadcast | Track weekly |

**Growth Tactics**:
- Every transaction = WA number captured
- "Simpan nomor WA kami" as part of onboarding
- Review cards with WA QR code
- All social CTAs drive to WA

**Integration**: Every IG/TikTok post CTA → WA. Every campaign ends at WA.

---

## Instagram — Brand Building + Engagement

**Role**: Build brand awareness, showcase quality, drive interest to WA for conversion.

**Content Types**:
| Type | Format | Frequency |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Edukasi | Reels (30–60s) | 1x/week |
| Social Proof | Reels, Carousel, Stories | 1x/week |
| Promo | Reels (15–30s), Stories | 1x/week |
| Behind the Scene | Reels, Stories | 1x/week |
| Interaktif | Stories (polls, Q&A) | 2x/week |

**Frequency**: 3 feed posts/week + daily stories when possible

**KPIs**:
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Followers | +50/month |
| Reach per post | >500 |
| Engagement rate | >5% |
| Profile visits/week | >100 |
| Link in bio clicks | Track weekly |

**Growth Tactics**:
- Use location tags (Boyolali) on every post
- Engage with local accounts (comment, like, follow)
- Use mix of popular + niche hashtags (see `brand/tone-of-voice.md`)
- Repost customer stories/tags
- Cross-post TikTok content (adapt for IG format)

**IG-Specific Notes**:
- Hashtags: 15–20 per post
- Caption: longer, keyword-rich, storytelling OK
- Aesthetic: slightly polished, brand colors visible
- Stories: use interactive stickers (poll, quiz, question)

## TikTok — Top of Funnel Reach

**Role**: Maximum reach and discovery. Attract new audience. Go viral.

**Content Types**:
| Type | Format | Frequency |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Satisfying/Transformation | 15–30s video | 1x/week |
| Edukasi | 30–60s video | 1x/week |
| POV/Relatable | 30–60s video | 1x/week |
| Challenge/Trend | 15–30s video | When relevant |

**Frequency**: 3 videos/week minimum

**KPIs**:
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Followers | +100/month |
| Average views | >1,000/video |
| Viral hits (>10K views) | 2/month |
| Profile visits | Track weekly |
| Bio link clicks | Track weekly |

**Growth Tactics**:
- Use trending sounds (check FYP daily)
- Post at peak times: 11AM–1PM, 7PM–9PM (Indonesian users)
- Hook in first 1–2 seconds (faster than IG)
- Reply to comments with video responses
- Participate in trending challenges with laundry twist
- Use TikTok-native features (stitch, duet, green screen)

**TikTok-Specific Notes**:
- Captions: short, punchy, 3–5 hashtags only
- Raw/authentic feel outperforms polished content
- Original audio can go viral — don't always use music
- Text overlay hooks are essential (many watch without sound)
- Trend speed: act within 48 hours of spotting a trend

## Google Maps — Local Trust Engine

**Role**: Build trust for people actively searching for laundry in Boyolali. Social proof through reviews.

**Content Types**:
- Weekly update post (promo, tip, or announcement)
- Photo uploads (outlet, machines, results, team)
- Review responses (all reviews within 24 hours)

**Frequency**: 1 post/week, photos monthly, reviews responded daily

**KPIs**:
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Total reviews | 100 in 3 months |
| Rating | Maintain 4.5+ |
| Photo views | Track monthly |
| Direction requests | Track monthly |
| Phone calls from Maps | Track monthly |

**Growth Tactics**:
- Review incentive cards in every order (QR code + Rp 2,000 discount)
- Staff trained to ask for reviews at pickup
- Follow-up WA 1 day after order: polite review request
- Post weekly with promo or tip
- Upload 10+ new photos per month (clean outlet, machines, results)
- Optimize description with keywords: "laundry Boyolali", "self service laundry", "laundry kilat Boyolali"
- Respond to every review (positive: thank, negative: solve)

## Channel Integration Rules

1. **All channels drive to WhatsApp** — WA is the conversion point
2. **Content flows**: TikTok (reach) → IG (brand) → WA (convert) → Google Maps (review)
3. **Cross-posting**: Film once, edit twice (TikTok version + IG version)
4. **Messaging consistency**: Same campaign message across all channels, adapted for format
5. **Promo announcements**: WA broadcast first, then IG post, then TikTok content
6. **Customer journey tracking**: Ask "tau Star Laundry dari mana?" at first visit
