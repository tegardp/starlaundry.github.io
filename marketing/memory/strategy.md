## Seasonal Learnings

### [2026-03-20] Pre-Lebaran 2026 — demand curve + service mix

**Context**: Lebaran holiday 19–23 Mar 2026. Data covers Jan 1 – Mar 18.
**Findings**:
- Revenue dipped from W10 onwards as mudik season began
- W12 (last 3 days before holiday) had strong daily avg (Rp 1.73M) — last-minute rush
- "Cuci Setrika Wangi 2 Hari" remained top service throughout — people want clothes ready before travel
- New customer acquisition was already low (27/week in W11) — people in travel mode, not trying new services
**For next year**: Start "Cuci Bersih Sebelum Mudik" campaign 2 weeks before. Push express services for last-minute customers.

## Funnel Stage Insights

### [2026-03-20] Retention strong, acquisition is the bottleneck

**Context**: Q1 2026 transaction data
**Findings**:
- Retention/repeat: 60.8% — healthy, not the problem
- Acquisition: declining from 117/week → 27/week — THIS is the bottleneck
- Conversion (payment): 98.4% — excellent, no leak here
- Reactivation: 112 belum diambil orders — small but easy win
**Action**: Allocate 60% of post-Lebaran marketing effort to top-of-funnel acquisition. Retention programs (stamp card, referral) are working — maintain but don't over-invest.
