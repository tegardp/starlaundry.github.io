## Audience Insights

### [2026-03-20] Customer base is 60.8% repeat — retention is strong

**Context**: Transaction analysis Jan 1 – Mar 18, 2026 (742 unique customers, 3,040 invoices)
**Insight**: 451 of 742 customers came back 2+ times. 198 customers (26.7%) visited 5+ times. Top customer visited 35 times in 77 days.
**Action**: Marketing should lean into loyalty programs (stamp card, referral) alongside acquisition. Returning customers are the revenue backbone.
**Evidence**: `analytics/transaction-report-q1-2026.md` — Customer Analysis section

### [2026-03-20] New customer acquisition declining — needs intervention

**Context**: Weekly new customer tracking Jan–Mar 2026
**Insight**: New customers dropped from 94–117/week (W01–W02) to 27–36/week (W09–W12). Return rate climbed to 88%, but total volume depends on fresh inflow.
**Action**: Post-Lebaran campaign must prioritize acquisition (Cuci Pertama promo, referral push, local content). Target: 60+ new customers/week.
**Evidence**: New vs Returning table in transaction report

### [2026-03-20] Self-service is the anak kos gateway

**Context**: Service mix analysis — Self Service = 13.9% of revenue (Rp 13.4M), 690+ orders
**Insight**: Self-service (cuci saja, kering saja) has low per-order value (Rp 10–14K) but high frequency. These are likely anak kos/mahasiswa. Gateway to upsell to Cuci Lipat or Cuci Setrika.
**Action**: Content targeting anak kos should feature self-service first, then show upgrade path. "Mulai dari Rp 10rb aja."
**Evidence**: Service category breakdown in transaction report

## Brand & Business Learnings

### [2026-03-20] Weekend is the money zone

**Context**: Day-of-week revenue analysis, 77 operating days
**Insight**: Jumat–Minggu = 47.4% of all transactions (1,472/3,040). Sabtu is #1 (508 txn, Rp 16.1M). Kamis is the slowest (350 txn, Rp 11.2M).
**Action**: Schedule best content to drop Thu evening/Fri morning to drive weekend traffic. Consider "Kamis Hemat" promo to fill the gap.

### [2026-03-20] Morning rush dominates (07:00–10:59)

**Context**: Hourly transaction distribution
**Insight**: 40.3% of daily transactions happen before 11:00. Afternoon 13:00–15:00 is the dead zone.
**Action**: Post content early morning (06:00–07:00) to catch pre-visit scrollers. Consider afternoon promos ("Titip Siang, Sore Beres").

### [2026-03-20] Express services = premium upsell opportunity

**Context**: Per-order revenue comparison across speed tiers
**Insight**: Cuci Setrika 6 Jam averages Rp 57,346/order vs Rp 31,771 for 2 Hari. Express is 1.8x more valuable per order.
**Action**: Content should normalize express ("Buru-buru? 6 jam aja cukup"). Upsell at counter when customers arrive.

### [2026-03-20] Delivery (Antar Jemput) is almost unused

**Context**: Only Rp 45,000 total delivery revenue in 77 days
**Insight**: Despite being a key differentiator vs traditional laundry, almost nobody uses delivery. Either awareness is low or process is unclear.
**Action**: Dedicated content series about Antar Jemput. Make it dead simple: "WA aja, kita jemput." Test if it's an awareness or process problem.

## Seasonal Patterns

### [2026-03-20] Pre-Lebaran demand drops ~2 weeks before

**Context**: W11 (Mar 9–15) revenue Rp 7.8M vs W06–W10 average Rp 9.5M. W12 (Mar 16–18, 3 days) = Rp 5.2M.
**Insight**: Demand starts declining ~1 week before Lebaran as people travel mudik. W12 had strong daily avg (Rp 1.73M/day) suggesting last-minute rush before holiday.
**Action**: For Lebaran 2027 — push "cuci bersih sebelum mudik" campaign 2 weeks before. Expect slowdown but capitalize on last-minute demand.
