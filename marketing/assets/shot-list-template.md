## Shot Type Reference

| Type | Description | Best For |
|------|-------------|----------|
| **Wide** | Full room/area visible, establishes context | Outlet tours, establishing shots, "before" shots |
| **Medium** | Waist-up framing of person, or half of a workspace | Talking head, demonstrations, staff introductions |
| **Close-up** | Face, hands, or item fills 70% of frame | Reactions, detail work, emotional moments |
| **Detail/Macro** | Item fills entire frame, very close | Fabric texture, stains, buttons, steam, machine dials |
| **Over-the-shoulder** | Camera behind talent, showing what they see | Process demonstrations, folding, ironing |
| **Bird's eye** | Top-down angle looking straight down | Flat-lay of folded clothes, packing, sorting |
| **Low angle** | Camera pointing up at subject | Makes machines look impressive, dramatic reveals |

## Camera Movement Reference

| Movement | Description | Best For |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| **Static** | Phone on tripod, no movement | Talking head, detail shots, ASMR |
| **Pan** | Horizontal sweep left or right | Revealing a space, showing a row of machines |
| **Tilt** | Vertical sweep up or down | Revealing a pile (tilt up), showing full outfit (tilt down) |
| **Tracking** | Following a person or object | Walking through outlet, following laundry bag |
| **Handheld** | Natural hand movement, slightly shaky | BTS, authentic/raw content, TikTok style |
| **Zoom in** | Moving closer to subject (digital or physical) | Emphasis, reveals, reactions |
| **Zoom out** | Moving away from subject | Reveals, showing the full picture |
