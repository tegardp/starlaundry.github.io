# Editing Guidelines for CapCut — Star Laundry Boyolali

These guidelines ensure every video maintains consistent quality and brand identity. All editing is done in CapCut (free version).

---

## General Principles

1. **Bright and clean** — every video should feel fresh, modern, and hygienic
2. **Mobile-first** — everything must be readable and impactful on a phone screen
3. **Brand-consistent** — Star Blue (#2AADE0) and Star Yellow (#FBB818) appear in every video
4. **Platform-aware** — edit differently for Instagram Reels vs TikTok (see platform-specific sections below)

---

## Pacing Rules

### Standard Pacing
| Content Type | Cut Frequency | Total Duration |
|--------------|---------------|----------------|
| Hook/intro | New visual every 0.5-1 sec | 1-3 sec |
| Educational/tutorial | Cut every 2-3 sec | 30-45 sec |
| Satisfying/transformation | Cut every 2-4 sec (let it breathe) | 15-30 sec |
| POV/relatable | Cut every 1-2 sec | 15-25 sec |
| Promo announcement | Cut every 1-2 sec | 15-20 sec |
| BTS/day-in-life | Cut every 2-3 sec | 30-60 sec |

### Pacing Principles
- **First 3 seconds:** Fastest cuts. This is where you win or lose the viewer
- **Middle section:** Slightly slower, deliver value
- **Final 3 seconds:** Slow down for CTA — give viewer time to read and act
- **Never hold a static shot for more than 4 seconds** unless it is intentionally satisfying (ironing ASMR, folding)
- **Speed ramps:** Use 2x-4x speed for process shots (loading machine, waiting). Never show real-time waiting

---

## Cut Rhythm

### Beat-Synced Editing
- For music-driven content, align cuts to the beat of the track
- Major cuts on strong beats (kick drum), minor adjustments on off-beats
- Use CapCut's "Auto beat" feature to mark beats, then align clips

### Jump Cuts
- Acceptable for TikTok content — gives energy and authenticity
- For Instagram: use smooth transitions instead of hard jump cuts when possible
- When jump-cutting talking head content: cut on the word, not between words

### Transition Types by Purpose
| Purpose | Transition | CapCut Effect |
|---------|-----------|---------------|
| Standard scene change | Smooth cut or dissolve | "Dissolve" (0.3 sec) |
| Before/after reveal | Swipe or wipe | "Slide Left" or "Wipe" |
| Energetic/fast content | Hard cut (no transition) | Direct cut |
| Process sequence | Zoom through | "Zoom Blur" (0.2 sec) |
| End card appearance | Fade to blue/brand card | "Fade" into solid color |

### Transitions to Avoid
- Star wipe, circle wipe, or any "PowerPoint" style transitions
- Overly long transitions (keep under 0.5 sec)
- Inconsistent transitions within one video — pick one style and stick with it
- 3D or spin transitions unless intentionally comedic

## Text Animation Style

### Hook Text
- **Animation in:** Pop-in or scale-up (0.2-0.3 sec)
- **Duration on screen:** 1.5-2.5 sec
- **Animation out:** Fade out or cut with next shot
- **Font:** Bold sans-serif, ALL CAPS
- **Color:** White with black outline (2-3px) for readability
- **Size:** Large — must be readable without squinting

### Subtitle/Explanation Text
- **Animation in:** Fade in (0.2 sec)
- **Duration on screen:** Match the voiceover/narration duration
- **Animation out:** Fade out (0.2 sec)
- **Font:** Regular sans-serif
- **Color:** White on semi-transparent dark background bar
- **Position:** Lower third, centered

### CTA Text
- **Animation in:** Slide up from bottom (0.3 sec)
- **Duration on screen:** Hold for full CTA duration (3-4 sec)
- **Font:** Bold sans-serif
- **Color:** Star Yellow (#FBB818) on Star Blue (#2AADE0) background
- **Include:** WhatsApp icon or number

### Price/Promo Text
- **Animation in:** Scale up with bounce effect
- **Font:** Extra bold
- **Color:** Star Yellow for the number, white for the unit (e.g., "/kg")
- **Background:** Star Blue rounded rectangle badge
- **Example:** "Rp 7.000" (yellow, large) "/kg" (white, smaller)

## Color Grading

### Standard Grade (apply to every video)
| Parameter | CapCut Setting |
|-----------|----------------|
| Brightness | +10 to +15 |
| Contrast | +5 to +10 |
| Saturation | +5 to +10 |
| Warmth | +5 (slightly warm, not orange) |
| Highlights | +5 |
| Shadows | +10 (lift shadows to keep it bright) |
| Sharpness | +10 to +15 |

### Style Notes
- **Bright and clean** — this is a laundry business, everything should look fresh
- Whites should look WHITE, not yellow or grey
- Blues should POP — boost blue saturation slightly to make brand elements vivid
- Avoid dark, moody, or desaturated grades
- If using a CapCut filter as a base, choose "Natural", "Clean", or "Fresh" — then adjust manually
- Apply the same grade to all clips in one video for consistency

### Grade by Content Type
| Content Type | Grade Adjustment |
|--------------|------------------|
| Satisfying/ASMR | Slightly more contrast, boost whites |
| BTS/authentic | Lighter grade, more natural |
| Promo/announcement | Vibrant, saturated |
| Educational | Clean, neutral |

## Audio Mixing

### Music vs Voiceover Levels
| Element | Level |
|---------|-------|
| Background music (no voiceover) | 80-100% |
| Background music (with voiceover) | 15-25% |
| Voiceover | 100% |
| Natural/ambient sound (ASMR) | 80-100% |
| Sound effects | 60-80% |

### Audio Rules
- **Music must not overpower voiceover.** If you can't clearly hear every word, turn the music down
- **Fade music in** at the start (0.5 sec fade-in)
- **Fade music out** at the end (1 sec fade-out) — never let it cut abruptly
- **Match energy:** upbeat music for energetic content, chill music for satisfying/ASMR
- **Audio levels should be consistent** across clips — normalize audio in CapCut
- For ASMR content: reduce music to 0-10%, let natural sounds dominate

### Sound Effects
- Use sparingly and purposefully
- Good uses: "ding" for reveals, "whoosh" for transitions, cash register for prices
- Bad uses: random sounds that don't add meaning, too many effects in one video
- CapCut's built-in sound effects library is sufficient

## Thumbnail Creation

### Instagram Reels — Custom Cover Image
Every IG Reel MUST have a custom cover image for profile grid consistency.

**How to create in CapCut:**
1. Export the best frame from the video as an image
2. Open in CapCut or Canva
3. Add text overlay (2-4 words, the hook or title)
4. Add Star Laundry branding (small logo or blue accent)
5. Ensure text is readable at thumbnail size
6. Save as 1080x1920

**Cover image checklist:**
- [ ] Bold, readable text (3-5 words max)
- [ ] Visually appealing background image
- [ ] Brand colors present (blue accent or text)
- [ ] Consistent style with other covers on the profile
- [ ] No text in the bottom 20% (covered by IG UI)

### TikTok — First Frame
TikTok uses the first frame or a selected frame as the cover.
- Select the most visually compelling frame
- If adding text, keep it large and centered
- Less polished than IG — authenticity is fine

## Watermark Handling

### Adding Star Laundry Watermark
- Place a small, semi-transparent Star Laundry logo in the **bottom-right corner**
- Opacity: 30-50% (visible but not distracting)
- Size: approximately 10-15% of frame width
- Present throughout the video (not just end card)
- This is OPTIONAL for TikTok (TikTok adds its own watermark)
- RECOMMENDED for Instagram Reels

### Removing Other Watermarks
- If repurposing TikTok content for Instagram: remove the TikTok watermark (use CapCut crop or re-export from original project)
- Never post content with another platform's watermark — Instagram deprioritizes videos with TikTok watermarks
- Always edit from the original CapCut project, not from a downloaded platform version
