# Visual Identity Guidelines — Star Laundry Boyolali

These guidelines ensure consistent visual identity across all marketing content — social media posts, reels, stories, print materials, and in-outlet signage.

---

## Brand Colors

| Color | Hex Code | Usage |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Star Blue** | `#2AADE0` | Primary brand color. Headlines, text overlays, backgrounds, CTA buttons |
| **Star Yellow** | `#FBB818` | Accent color. Highlights, promo badges, secondary elements |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Backgrounds, text on dark/blue backgrounds |
| Dark Gray | `#333333` | Body text, subtitles |
| Light Gray | `#F5F5F5` | Soft backgrounds, cards |

### Color Usage Rules
- **Blue #2AADE0** is always dominant — it should be the first color a viewer associates with Star Laundry
- **Yellow #FBB818** is used for emphasis — promo badges, price callouts, "NEW" tags, highlights
- Never use blue and yellow in equal proportion — blue should occupy 70% of branded color space, yellow 30%
- Text on blue backgrounds: always white
- Text on white backgrounds: dark gray or blue
- Avoid red, green, or other colors that are not part of the palette (exception: seasonal campaigns like Ramadan green/gold — used sparingly)

---

## Typography

### On-Screen Text (Reels/TikTok)
- **Primary font:** Bold sans-serif (use CapCut built-in bold fonts like "Montserrat Bold" or "Poppins Bold")
- **Size:** Large enough to read on mobile — minimum 40pt equivalent in CapCut
- **Style:** ALL CAPS for hooks and key phrases, Title Case for subtitles
- **Color:** White with dark shadow/outline for readability on any background, OR Star Blue on white backgrounds
- **Position:** Upper third of frame for hooks, lower third for subtitles/CTAs
- **Animation:** Fade in or pop-in — avoid overly flashy animations that distract from the message

### Caption Text
- Use standard platform fonts
- Emoji usage: moderate, relevant (use cleaning/sparkle emojis, avoid random emojis)
- Structure with line breaks for readability

---

## Logo Usage

### Placement in Videos
- **End card:** Always include Star Laundry logo in the final 2-3 seconds
- **Corner watermark:** Small, semi-transparent logo in bottom-right corner during the video (optional but recommended for IG)
- **Do NOT** place the logo over the main action area
- **Do NOT** animate the logo with flashy effects

### Logo Clear Space
- Always maintain clear space around the logo — no text or graphics touching it
- On busy backgrounds, place the logo on a semi-transparent white or blue card

## Photo & Video Style

### Overall Aesthetic
- **Bright** — well-lit, no dark or moody shots (exception: dramatic before/after reveals)
- **Clean** — organized backgrounds, no clutter in frame
- **Modern** — contemporary feel matching the coin laundry concept
- **Warm** — slightly warm white balance, not cold/clinical

### Color Grading (Video)
- Boost brightness +10-15%
- Increase contrast slightly +5-10%
- Warm the white balance slightly (not orange, just warm-clean)
- Boost saturation on blues to make Star Blue elements pop
- Keep whites clean (not yellowish)
- CapCut filter recommendation: use "Clean" or "Fresh" style filters, then adjust manually

### What to Avoid
- Dark, underexposed footage
- Yellow/green color casts from fluorescent lighting
- Overly filtered or vintage looks
- Grainy footage (clean the camera lens before filming)

## Lighting Guidance

### Primary: Natural Light
- Film near the outlet windows whenever possible
- Best time: 8:00-10:00 AM and 2:00-4:00 PM (avoid harsh midday sun)
- Position talent facing the window, camera between talent and window
- Use a white surface (towel, paper) as a simple reflector to fill shadows

### Secondary: Outlet Lighting
- Ensure all outlet lights are ON during filming
- The overhead fluorescent lights provide even illumination
- Supplement with phone flash or a small ring light for close-ups

### For Specific Shots
| Shot Type | Lighting Approach |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Talking head | Face the window, ring light as fill |
| Machine close-up | Outlet overhead lights, phone flash if needed |
| Transformation reveal | Well-lit for the "after" shot — make it glow |
| ASMR/detail shots | Soft, even light — avoid harsh shadows |
| Outlet tour | All lights on, film during daytime for window light |

## Framing Guidance for Phone Filming

### Aspect Ratio
- **Reels/TikTok:** Always 9:16 vertical (1080x1920)
- **Stories:** 9:16 vertical
- **Feed posts:** 4:5 or 1:1 (if needed)
- Lock your phone orientation — no accidental horizontal footage

### Composition Rules
- **Rule of thirds:** Place the subject at 1/3 intersections, not dead center
- **Headroom:** Leave space above the subject's head for text overlays (hooks go in the upper 20% of frame)
- **Safe zones:** Keep important visual elements out of the top 15% and bottom 20% of frame (covered by platform UI)
- **Clean backgrounds:** Check the background before filming — remove clutter, close cabinets, tidy up

### Phone Filming Tips
- Clean the camera lens before every session
- Use the back camera (higher quality) with a tripod or phone mount
- Stabilize: lean against something solid or use a mini tripod
- Film in 1080p or 4K if storage allows
- Tap to focus and lock exposure before starting

### Shot Types Reference
| Shot Type | When to Use | Framing |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| Wide | Outlet tours, context shots | Full room/area visible |
| Medium | Talking head, demonstrations | Waist up |
| Close-up | Detail work, reactions, folding | Hands/face/item fills 70% of frame |
| Detail/Macro | Fabric texture, stains, steam | Item fills entire frame |
| Over-the-shoulder | Process demonstrations | Behind talent, showing their hands |

## Thumbnail Design Guidelines

### Instagram Reels Cover Images
- Design a custom cover image for every Reel (do not rely on auto-generated frames)
- Use Canva or CapCut to create covers
- Include: bold text (hook/title), Star Laundry branding, visually appealing image
- Text should be readable at small size (3-5 words maximum)
- Use Star Blue background or overlay with white text
- Maintain consistent style across all covers for a cohesive profile grid

### TikTok
- The first frame IS the thumbnail — make it visually compelling
- If using a custom cover, keep text large and centered
- Less designed than IG — authenticity matters more

## On-Screen Text Styling

### Hook Text (First 1-3 seconds)
- Font: Bold sans-serif, ALL CAPS
- Size: Large (fills 60-80% of frame width)
- Color: White with black outline/shadow
- Position: Center or upper third
- Animation: Pop-in or fade-in (0.3 sec)
- Example: "JANGAN CUCI BAJU KAYAK GINI!"

### Subtitle/Explanation Text
- Font: Regular sans-serif, Title Case
- Size: Medium (readable but not dominant)
- Color: White with dark background strip, or Star Blue on white
- Position: Lower third
- Animation: Fade in, stay for 2-3 sec, fade out

### CTA Text (Final seconds)
- Font: Bold sans-serif
- Size: Large
- Color: Star Yellow on Star Blue background, or White on Blue
- Position: Center of frame
- Include: WA icon/number, "Link di bio", or action text
- Animation: Slide up or pop-in

### Price/Promo Text
- Font: Extra bold
- Size: Extra large for the price number
- Color: Star Yellow for the price, White for context
- Background: Star Blue card/badge
- Example: "Rp 7.000/kg" in yellow on blue badge

## Color Overlay Usage

### When to Use Overlays
- To darken footage for text readability
- To add brand color tint to transitions
- To create consistent look across different lighting conditions

### Overlay Specifications
| Purpose | Color | Opacity |
|---------|-------|---------|
| Text readability (dark) | Black #000000 | 30-40% |
| Brand tint | Star Blue #2AADE0 | 10-15% |
| Promo highlight | Star Yellow #FBB818 | 15-20% |
| End card background | Star Blue #2AADE0 | 90-100% |

### Rules
- Never use color overlays that make footage look unnatural
- Dark overlays only behind text, not full-frame
- Brand tint overlays should be subtle — the viewer should barely notice
- End cards can be solid Star Blue with white text/logo
