═══ OBJECTIVE ═══
Primary: Restore daily transaction volume to pre-Lebaran levels (39+ txn/day, Rp 1.25M+/day) within 1 week
Secondary: Acquire 60+ new customers/week (back to W04 levels from current 27/week decline)
Tertiary: Activate delivery service (Antar Jemput) — target 20+ delivery orders in 3 weeks
Why now: 5-day Lebaran closure (19–23 Mar). Customers have piled-up laundry from mudik.
         New customer acquisition was declining pre-Lebaran — this is the natural reset moment.

═══ TARGET SEGMENT ═══
Primary: Keluarga Muda — returning from mudik with bags of dirty clothes

═══ OFFER ═══

Phase 1: "Welcome Back" (24–30 Mar, Week 1)
  What: Cuci Pertama Rp 3,000/kg for NEW customers (existing promo, amplified)
        + Bonus stamp card: start with 2 stamps (instead of 0) for returning customers
  Terms: All service types, no minimum kg
  Mechanics: Mention "Promo Lebaran" at drop-off or via WA
  Stacking: Can combine with Senin Hemat on 24 Mar (double incentive on reopening day)

Phase 2: "Antar Jemput Gratis" (31 Mar–6 Apr, Week 2)
  What: Free delivery for orders 5kg+ within 3km (normally free only for first-time)
  Terms: Min 5kg, max 3km radius, WA order only
  Mechanics: WA "JEMPUT" + alamat → staff picks up
  Stacking: Yes, with all other promos
  Why: Delivery usage is near-zero (Rp 45K total in Q1). This tests if awareness or process is the blocker.

Phase 3: "Ajak Teman" Referral Push (7–13 Apr, Week 3)
  What: Rp 5,000 off for BOTH referrer and friend (existing promo, amplified)
        + Extra: if friend uses service within 7 days, referrer gets bonus 2 stamps
  Terms: Friend must be new customer
  Mechanics: Referrer gives friend their phone number at drop-off. Staff validates.
