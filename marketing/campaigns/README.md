# Campaign System — Star Laundry Boyolali

## Overview

This folder contains all marketing campaigns for Star Laundry. A campaign is a coordinated set of marketing activities around a specific theme, goal, or time period. Campaigns bundle together content, offers, messaging, and distribution into a cohesive push.

## Types of Campaigns

### 1. Launch Campaigns
**Purpose:** Introduce something new — a new service, new location, new feature, or the business itself.
**Duration:** 2-4 weeks
**Intensity:** High (daily content, multiple channels)
**Examples:**
- Grand opening campaign
- New pickup/delivery service launch
- New self-service hours launch

**Folder:** `launch-campaigns/`

### 2. Evergreen Campaigns
**Purpose:** Always-on campaigns that run continuously or are reusable year-round. Not tied to a specific date.
**Duration:** Ongoing / reusable
**Intensity:** Low-medium (woven into regular content)
**Examples:**
- "First Time? Gratis 3 KG" — new customer acquisition
- "Referral Reward" — word of mouth program
- "Langganan Star Laundry" — loyalty/retention campaign

**Folder:** `evergreen-campaigns/`

### 3. Seasonal Campaigns
**Purpose:** Time-bound campaigns tied to holidays, seasons, or cultural moments.
**Duration:** 1-4 weeks
**Intensity:** Medium-high (dedicated content burst)
**Examples:**
- Ramadan / Lebaran (cuci gordyn, baju lebaran)
- Back to School (cuci seragam)
- Musim Hujan (dryer promotion)
- Year-End / Natal & Tahun Baru
- Valentine's Day (cuci berdua promo)

**Folder:** `seasonal-campaigns/`

## How to Plan a Campaign

### Step 1: Define the Campaign
- What is the objective? (awareness, trial, retention, revenue)
- Who is the target segment?
- What is the offer or key message?
- What is the timeline?

### Step 2: Create the Campaign Brief
- Use `../assets/creative-brief-template.md` for overall creative direction
- Define messaging in Indonesian (Bahasa)
- Set KPI targets

### Step 3: Plan Content
- Map content pieces to the timeline (week by week)
- Assign content to platforms (IG, TikTok, WA, Google Maps)
- Create short-form briefs for each video using `../shortform/templates/brief-template.md`
- Write scripts and captions

### Step 4: Produce and Schedule
- Batch-film content for the campaign
- Edit per `../assets/editing-guidelines.md`
- Schedule using platform schedulers

### Step 5: Execute and Monitor
- Publish per schedule
- Monitor engagement daily
- Adjust if underperforming

### Step 6: Review
- Post-campaign review against KPIs
- Document learnings
- Archive campaign materials

## Naming Convention

Campaign files are named using lowercase kebab-case:
- `ramadan-lebaran.md`
- `grand-opening.md`
- `first-time-free.md`
- `musim-hujan-promo.md`

## Campaign Lifecycle

```
Planning → Brief → Content Production → Scheduling → Live → Monitoring → Review → Archive
   │                                                                          │
   └──────────────── Learnings feed back into next campaign ──────────────────┘
```

### Timeline Rules of Thumb
| Campaign Type | Lead Time | Content Volume |
|---------------|-----------|----------------|
| Launch | 3-4 weeks before launch | 15-20 pieces |
| Seasonal | 2-3 weeks before season | 10-15 pieces |
| Evergreen | 1 week to set up | 5-8 pieces (then ongoing) |
