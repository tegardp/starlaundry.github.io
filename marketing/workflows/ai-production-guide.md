## 1. PRODUCTION APPROACH RECOMMENDATION

### The Golden Rule for Local Businesses

**80% real footage, 20% AI-assisted enhancement.** Not the other way around.

Star Laundry is a local, trust-based service business in Boyolali. Customers will come because they trust real people, a real place, and real results. Fully AI-generated content creates an uncanny valley effect that actively damages credibility for local businesses. A potential customer scrolling Instagram who sees an AI-generated "laundry shop" that does not match the real shop on Jalan XYZ will feel deceived.

### What Should Be REAL Footage

| Element | Why It Must Be Real |
|---|---|
| The actual shop exterior and interior | Customers need to recognize it when they visit |
| The washing machines and equipment | Proves you have real, working machines |
| Staff faces and interactions | Builds personal trust (this is Boyolali, people know people) |
| Before/after laundry results | The core proof of your service quality |
| The delivery process (pickup/dropoff) | Shows the real service in action |
| Customer testimonials or reactions | Authenticity is everything here |
| Day-in-the-life footage | The whole point is showing reality |

### Where AI Adds Value (the 20%)

| Element | Why AI Works Here |
|---|---|
| Text overlays and animated captions | CapCut AI handles this perfectly |
| Background music selection and beat-sync | AI music tools save hours of searching |
| Voiceover polish (noise reduction, enhancement) | Improves phone-recorded audio quality |
| Transition effects between scenes | CapCut templates + AI transitions |
| Thumbnail and cover image design | AI-assisted design for eye-catching covers |
| B-roll filler (generic laundry close-ups) | Only when real footage has gaps |
| Auto-subtitles in Bahasa Indonesia | CapCut auto-caption is excellent for this |
| Script-to-storyboard visualization | Planning aid, not final content |
| Color correction and grading | One-tap filters to unify visual style |
| Speed ramping and pacing | AI-suggested cuts save editing time |

### Credibility Risks of Full AI Content

1. **Visual mismatch**: AI-generated shop interiors will not match your real shop. When a customer visits and it looks different, trust is broken immediately.
2. **Cultural inaccuracy**: AI models are trained primarily on Western data. Generated images of "Indonesian laundry" often include wrong details -- wrong signage style, wrong clothing types, wrong neighborhood aesthetics.
3. **The "too polished" problem**: In Boyolali, content that looks like it was made by a Jakarta ad agency feels disconnected. Slightly imperfect, genuine footage performs better than sterile perfection.
4. **Platform detection**: Both Instagram and TikTok are increasingly flagging and deprioritizing AI-generated content. Real footage gets better algorithmic treatment.
5. **Community trust**: In a small town, word spreads. If someone recognizes your content as fake, it damages reputation beyond just one video.

### The Hybrid Strategy

```
LAYER 1 (Foundation):  Real phone footage of shop, staff, machines, results
LAYER 2 (Enhancement): CapCut AI features for captions, transitions, color
LAYER 3 (Filler):      AI-generated B-roll ONLY for abstract/generic shots
LAYER 4 (Audio):       Mix of real voice + AI-enhanced audio + AI/library music
```

### 2E. AI Talking Head / Avatar

#### Honest Assessment: SKIP THIS for Star Laundry

- **Why**: AI talking heads (HeyGen, D-ID, etc.) look obviously artificial. For a local business in Boyolali where customers might walk in and meet the real staff, using a digital avatar creates a jarring disconnect.
- **The uncanny valley problem**: Current AI avatars still have telltale signs -- unnatural eye movement, stiff lip sync, plastic-looking skin. This is especially noticeable to Indonesian audiences who are very tuned in to facial expressions and body language in conversation.
- **What to do instead**: Film the real owner or a staff member. Even a phone recording with average lighting will outperform the most polished AI avatar for local business trust.
- **One exception**: If you absolutely cannot film a person and need a "host" presence, CapCut's built-in avatar feature is the cheapest option. But use it as a last resort, not a first choice.

### REEL 1: "Buka Lagi! Tumpukan Baju Mudik"

**Concept**: POV opening suitcase full of dirty clothes after mudik (holiday travel) then bringing them to Star Laundry for transformation.
**Duration**: 30 seconds

#### What to Film with Phone (Real Footage)

1. **POV opening a suitcase** (5s): Film from above, hands opening a suitcase stuffed with wrinkled, dirty clothes. Use a real suitcase, stuff it dramatically. Film in natural indoor light.
2. **Overwhelmed reaction shot** (2s): Staff member or owner making a "oh no!" face, looking at the pile.
3. **Walking into Star Laundry** (3s): Film someone carrying a laundry bag through the real shop entrance. Get the signage in frame.
4. **Handing clothes to staff / loading machine** (3s): Real interaction at the counter or at a machine.
5. **Machine running** (3s): Close-up of the real washing machine in motion. Get the spinning drum.
6. **Clean clothes reveal** (5s): Neatly folded, fresh clothes. Film with good lighting -- lay them on a clean surface, arrange nicely.
7. **Happy customer walking out** (3s): Someone carrying a clean bag, smiling, exiting the shop.

#### What to Generate with AI

- **Transition graphic**: A simple "before/after" split-screen wipe effect -- use CapCut template, no AI generation needed.
- **Text overlays**: "Buka Lagi!" hook text, "Tumpukan Baju Mudik" subtitle -- CapCut text animation.
- **Optional B-roll**: If the suitcase shot does not look dramatic enough, use Kling AI to generate a 3-second clip of "overflowing suitcase with wrinkled colorful clothes, POV top-down, dramatic reveal" as an alternative opening shot.

#### Voiceover Approach

- **Real voice preferred**. Record the owner or a staff member saying the narration on phone.
- Script line examples (record naturally, not robotic):
  - "Habis mudik, baju numpuk?"
  - "Bawa aja ke Star Laundry!"
  - "Bersih, wangi, siap pakai lagi!"
- If owner is camera-shy: use ElevenLabs with an Indonesian female voice, warm and friendly tone.
- Backup: CapCut TTS Indonesian voice.

#### Text Overlay Approach

- **0-3s**: Hook text "BUKA LAGI!" in large, bold font with bounce animation. Yellow text on dark overlay.
- **3-5s**: "Tumpukan baju mudik..." in smaller handwritten-style font.
- **15-20s**: "Star Laundry solusinya!" with the logo.
- **25-30s**: CTA "Cuci sekarang! Link di bio" with location pin icon.
- All text via CapCut text animation templates.

#### Music/Audio Approach

- **BGM**: Upbeat Indonesian pop or dangdut remix (search CapCut library: "happy Indonesian" or "upbeat folk"). Keep at -20dB under voice.
- **SFX**: Suitcase zip sound (opening), washing machine hum, "ding!" sparkle sound on clean clothes reveal.
- Source SFX from CapCut library or Pixabay.

#### Estimated Production Time

| Task | Time |
|---|---|
| Filming all clips | 30 minutes |
| Voiceover recording | 10 minutes |
| AI asset generation (if needed) | 15 minutes |
| Assembly in CapCut | 30 minutes |
| Text overlays + captions | 20 minutes |
| Audio mixing + SFX | 15 minutes |
| Review and export | 10 minutes |
| **Total** | **~2.5 hours** |

#### Step-by-Step Assembly in CapCut

1. Create new project: 9:16, 30fps.
2. Import all phone footage and sort by scene order.
3. Place clips on timeline in narrative order: suitcase open, reaction, walk to shop, counter, machine, clean reveal, exit.
4. Trim each clip to length (total ~25s of footage to leave room for text beats).
5. Add speed curve to machine clip (speed up the middle, slow at start/end).
6. Apply transition: hard cut between most clips, dissolve on the "clean reveal" moment.
7. Add voiceover track. Align narration to visual beats.
8. Add BGM track. Adjust volume to -20dB. Use Beat Sync to check alignment.
9. Add text overlays at scripted moments. Apply animation presets.
10. Add SFX (zip, machine, ding) at key moments.
11. Apply color filter (warm, slightly saturated -- "fresh and clean" look) uniformly to all clips.
12. Auto-generate captions for voiceover. Review for accuracy. Style them (white text, dark stroke, bottom-third position).
13. Preview full reel. Check audio levels, text readability, pacing.
14. Export: 1080x1920, 30fps, H.264, bitrate 10-15Mbps.

### REEL 2: "Cuci Pertama Cuma Rp 3rb/kg!"

**Concept**: Promo video showing the special first-wash price, the machines, and clean results.
**Duration**: 30 seconds

#### What to Film with Phone (Real Footage)

2. **Shop exterior with signage** (2s): Quick establishing shot showing Star Laundry is a real, operating shop.
3. **The machines** (3s): Pan across the washing machines. Show they are modern, clean, and running.
4. **Loading clothes into machine** (3s): Real action shot of clothes going in.
5. **Machine in action** (2s): Close-up of spinning drum or water and suds.
6. **Clean result** (3s): Take out fresh, clean clothes. Hold them up. Maybe smell them (the classic "fresh laundry" gesture).
7. **Neatly packaged laundry** (3s): Show the folded and bagged laundry ready for pickup. This is your "proof of quality."
8. **Staff smiling / waving** (2s): Friendly face to end the video. Builds personal connection.

#### What to Generate with AI

- **Price graphic animation**: Create in Canva -- a bold "Rp 3.000/kg" graphic with star burst effect, export as PNG, import to CapCut and animate with scale-bounce.

- **2-5s**: "Cuci Pertama PROMO!" with the stamp graphic.
- **10-15s**: "Mesin modern" text appears near the machines.

5. **"Reason 3 - Makin Males" visual** (4s): Person sitting on couch, looking at the growing pile, sighing. Relatable "malas" energy.

- **10-14s**: "#2 NODA MEMBANDEL" with stain close-up.
- **15-19s**: "#3 MAKIN MALES" with "malas" text effect (drooping text animation).
- **20-25s**: "SOLUSINYA?" then "STAR LAUNDRY" reveal.
- **30-35s**: CTA with address/WA.

#### Music/Audio Approach

- **BGM**: Slightly dramatic/suspenseful for the "problem" section (reasons 1-3), then switch to upbeat/positive for the "solution" section. CapCut has mood-transition tracks.
- **SFX**: Gross "squelch" sound for bacteria, "buzz" warning sound for each reason, triumphant "ta-da!" for the solution.

11. Color: slightly desaturated/cold for the "problem" sections, warm/bright for the "solution" section. This creates subconscious emotional contrast.
12. Auto-captions. Style consistently with other reels.
13. Export.

### REEL 4: "Males Keluar? WA Aja, Kita Jemput!"

**Concept**: Tutorial showing the delivery/pickup service step by step.
**Duration**: 30 seconds

#### What to Film with Phone (Real Footage)

1. **Opening hook** (3s): Person lying on couch/bed, surrounded by dirty laundry, looking lazy. Caption: "Males keluar rumah?"
2. **Phone screen recording** (5s): Screen-record the actual process of sending a WhatsApp message to Star Laundry's number. Show the real number, the real chat. This is crucial -- it proves the service exists and shows exactly how to use it.
3. **Staff receiving the message** (2s): Staff at the shop checking phone, nodding.
4. **Pickup scene** (4s): Staff/driver arriving at a house, picking up a laundry bag. Film at a real house -- even a staff member's house works.
5. **At the shop** (3s): Quick shot of the laundry being processed.
6. **Delivery back** (4s): Staff/driver returning with clean, packaged laundry.
7. **Happy customer** (3s): Person receiving the clean laundry at their door, smiling.

#### What to Generate with AI

## 4. WORKFLOW: START TO FINISH

This is the standard workflow for producing any single reel from script to published post.

---

### STEP 1: Pre-Production (30-45 minutes)

**1.1 Review the Script**
- Read the script beats, identify each visual moment.
- List every shot needed (create a simple shot list on paper or phone notes).
- Identify which shots are "must film" vs "can use AI/stock."

**1.2 Gather Assets**
- Check if you already have relevant footage from previous shoots.
- Download or create needed graphics in Canva (price tags, badges, step numbers).
- Identify the BGM -- search CapCut library and save/favorite 2-3 options.
- Charge phone, clear storage space (each reel needs 2-5 GB free for raw footage).

**1.3 Prepare the Location**
- Clean the shop area that will appear on camera.
- Check lighting -- shoot during daylight hours when possible (natural light from windows is your best friend).
- Prepare props: clean laundry for "after" shots, dirty laundry for "before" shots, stamp card, price signs, etc.

---

### STEP 2: Real Footage Capture (20-60 minutes)

**2.1 Phone Filming Tips**

- **Resolution**: 1080p at 30fps minimum. 4K if phone supports it (but 1080p is fine for social media).
- **Orientation**: ALWAYS VERTICAL (9:16). Lock rotation on your phone.
- **Lighting**:
  - Shoot facing a window for natural soft light (never with the window behind the subject -- that creates a silhouette).
  - Avoid overhead fluorescent lights (they create harsh, unflattering shadows and a green color cast).
  - If shooting at night, use a cheap ring light (Rp 50,000-100,000 on Shopee/Tokopedia).
- **Stability**:
  - Use a phone tripod (Rp 30,000-80,000 on Shopee/Tokopedia) for static shots.
  - For moving shots, hold phone with both hands, elbows tucked to body, walk smoothly.
  - CapCut has stabilization -- use it on shaky clips.
- **Audio**:
  - Record voice in a quiet environment.
  - Speak clearly toward the phone.
  - For better audio: clip-on lavalier mic (Rp 30,000-50,000 on Shopee/Tokopedia) plugs into phone.
- **Filming rules**:
  - Hold each shot for at least 5 seconds (gives editing room).
  - Film 2-3 takes of each shot (you want options).
  - Check the footage after each shot (no blurry disasters).
  - Film more than you think you need -- it is always easier to have extra footage than to reshoot.

**2.2 Shot Checklist per Reel**

Before filming, check off each shot from the shot list. After filming, review all footage to ensure you have everything before leaving the location.

---

### STEP 3: AI Asset Generation (15-30 minutes)

**3.1 Graphics in Canva**

1. Open Canva, search for "Instagram Reel" template (1080x1920).
2. Create needed graphics: price tags, step numbers, badges, stamp cards.
3. Use Star Laundry brand colors (if defined) or stick to blue/white/clean aesthetic.
4. Export each graphic as PNG with transparent background (Pro feature) or solid background.
5. Save to phone/computer for import to CapCut.

**3.2 AI B-Roll (if needed)**

1. Open Kling AI / PixVerse in browser.
2. Enter the prompt (see Section 5 for templates).
3. Generate 2-3 variations.
4. Download the best one.
5. Import to CapCut.

**3.3 AI Voiceover (if not using real voice)**

1. Open ElevenLabs or CapCut TTS.
2. Paste the script text.
3. Select Indonesian voice.
4. Adjust speed (slightly faster than default for short-form -- 1.1x to 1.2x).
5. Generate and download.
6. Import to CapCut.

**3.4 AI Music (if not using CapCut library)**

1. Open Suno AI.
2. Describe the mood: "Upbeat Indonesian pop, 120 BPM, 30 seconds, happy and energetic, no vocals."
3. Generate 2-3 options.
4. Download the best one.
5. Import to CapCut.

---

### STEP 4: Assembly in CapCut (25-40 minutes)

**4.1 Project Setup**
1. Open CapCut, create new project.
2. Set canvas to 9:16.
3. Import all assets: footage, graphics, voiceover, music.

**4.2 Rough Cut**
1. Place footage on timeline in narrative order.
2. Trim each clip to approximate length.
3. Watch through -- does the story make sense? Is the hook strong?
4. Total rough cut should be within 5 seconds of target duration.

**4.3 Fine Cut**
1. Frame-accurate trimming. Every cut should feel intentional.
2. Apply transitions: default to hard cuts. Use dissolves only for mood shifts. Use CapCut template transitions for 1-2 key moments (do not overuse).
3. Apply speed curves where scripted (timelapse, slow-mo, speed ramps).
4. Ensure the opening hook hits within the first 1-2 seconds. If not, restructure.

**4.4 Pacing Check**
- Play through at real speed. Does it feel too slow? Too rushed?
- Short-form video golden rule: if a moment feels slow, cut it shorter. Viewers will scroll past.
- For 30-second reels: aim for 10-15 cuts (2-3 seconds per shot average).
- For 45-second reels: aim for 15-20 cuts.

---

### STEP 5: Text Overlays and Captions (15-25 minutes)

**5.1 Auto-Captions**
1. In CapCut: Audio > Auto Captions > Select Bahasa Indonesia.
2. Generate. Wait for processing.
3. Review EVERY line. Fix errors (common mistakes: brand names, local terms, numbers).
4. Adjust timing -- each caption should appear slightly (0.1-0.2s) before the spoken word.
5. Choose caption style: white text, dark stroke, positioned at bottom third. Keep consistent across all 9 reels.

**5.2 Text Overlays (hook text, price callouts, CTAs)**
1. Add text elements at scripted moments.
2. Apply animation presets from CapCut templates:
   - Hook text: bounce-in or shake (bold, attention-grabbing).
   - Price/info text: scale-pop (appears crisp and clear).
   - CTA text: slide-in from bottom, stays for 3-4 seconds.
3. Font choice: Use one consistent font across all 9 reels. CapCut's built-in bold sans-serif fonts work well. Avoid decorative/cursive fonts for main text -- they are hard to read on phone screens.
4. Size: Hook text at 48-64px. Body text at 32-40px. CTA at 36-42px.
5. Position: Keep text out of the top 15% (IG username overlay area) and bottom 15% (caption area).

**5.3 Graphics Overlay**
1. Import Canva graphics (price tags, badges, step numbers).
2. Position on the timeline at the correct moments.
3. Apply animation: scale-bounce on entrance, hold for 2-3 seconds, fade on exit.
4. Ensure graphics do not overlap with text or cover important visual elements.

---

### STEP 6: Audio Mixing (10-15 minutes)

**6.1 Layer Order (top to bottom in priority)**
1. Voice/dialogue: -8dB to -6dB (loudest element).
2. Sound effects: -18dB to -14dB.
3. BGM: -22dB to -18dB when voice is present, -14dB to -10dB during voice gaps.

**6.2 Mixing Steps in CapCut**
1. Place voiceover on Audio Track 1.
2. Place BGM on Audio Track 2. Trim to video length. Add fade-in (0.5s) at start and fade-out (1s) at end.
3. Place SFX on Audio Track 3 at marked moments.
4. Adjust BGM volume: lower it during voiceover sections ("ducking"). CapCut Pro has an auto-ducking feature.
5. Play through with headphones. Check:
   - Can you clearly understand every word of the voiceover?
   - Are SFX audible but not startling?
   - Does the BGM enhance the mood without distracting?

**6.3 Voice Enhancement (if recorded on phone)**
1. Select the voiceover clip in CapCut.
2. Apply noise reduction (CapCut built-in): set to 50-70%. Do not max it out.
3. Apply voice enhancement if available.
4. Check: Does the voice sound clear? Is there a noticeable "underwater" effect from over-processing? Dial it back if so.

## 7. TIPS FOR LOCAL BUSINESS AI CONTENT

### How to Keep It Authentic and Not "AI-Looking"

1. **The 80/20 rule**: 80% of screen time should be real footage of real people, real machines, and real results. AI fills the remaining 20% -- transitions, graphics, enhancement, B-roll filler.

2. **Show the imperfections**: A slightly messy shop counter, a staff member with a genuine (not perfect) smile, natural lighting with shadows -- these "flaws" signal authenticity. AI-generated content is too perfect, and audiences subconsciously detect this.

3. **Feature real people**: The owner, the staff, real customers (with permission). Faces build trust. Boyolali is a community where people recognize each other. Seeing a real face makes the business approachable.

4. **Use AI for enhancement, not replacement**: AI should make your real footage look better (color correction, noise reduction, stabilization), not replace it entirely. Think of AI as a photo filter, not a photo replacement.

5. **Keep AI B-roll generic**: If you use AI-generated video clips, make them abstract -- water ripples, fabric close-ups, clean bubbles. Never use AI to generate shots of "your shop" or "your staff" because they will not match reality.

---

### Mixing Real and AI Content Seamlessly

1. **Color-match everything**: Apply the same color grade to both real footage and AI-generated clips. If your real footage has warm tones, add warm tones to AI clips too. Inconsistent color is the biggest giveaway.

2. **Match the motion quality**: If your real footage is slightly shaky (phone-held), adding a buttery-smooth AI clip next to it creates a jarring contrast. Add slight shake or stabilization to make them feel consistent.

3. **Use AI clips as inserts, not hero shots**: AI clips work best when they appear for 1-3 seconds between real footage. They should be "supporting detail" shots, not the main visual. The audience's eye should not linger long enough to analyze them.

4. **Consistent aspect ratio and resolution**: Generate all AI assets at 1080x1920 (9:16). If an AI tool only outputs landscape, crop it before importing. Mismatched framing breaks immersion.

5. **Add texture**: Apply a subtle grain or film texture to the entire video (both real and AI portions). This unifies the visual feel and masks the "too clean" look of AI-generated content. CapCut has grain overlays.

---

### What to Avoid

1. **AI-generated faces**: Do not use AI avatars or AI-generated human faces for a local business. They look fake, and customers will notice. Use real people.

2. **Wrong cultural cues**: AI models often generate "generic Asian" or "Western-style" laundry shops. Indonesian laundries have specific visual characteristics -- Indomie hanging nearby, ojol helmets at the counter, specific signage styles. If AI misses these details, the content feels foreign.

3. **Over-produced look**: A small-town laundry with cinema-grade visual effects looks suspicious. Keep the production quality consistent with what a small business would realistically produce. Professional but not Hollywood.

4. **AI-generated text in images**: AI is notoriously bad at generating readable text in images. Always add text in CapCut or Canva as an overlay, never baked into an AI-generated image.

5. **Trendy AI effects that age poorly**: Avoid using AI effects that scream "I used AI" -- like the AI art filters, AI cartoon effects, or obviously AI-generated music with telltale artifacts. These date your content and signal low effort.

6. **Inconsistent branding**: If Reel 1 has warm golden tones and Reel 2 has cool blue tones, the series feels disjointed. Establish one visual identity and apply it across all 9 reels. Use the same font, same color overlay, same caption style, same intro/outro music.

---

### How to Iterate and Improve Over Time

1. **Batch 1 (Reels 1-3)**: Focus on learning the workflow. Accept that the first batch will take longer and may not be perfect. The goal is to establish the process.

2. **Batch 2 (Reels 4-6)**: Template everything you repeated in Batch 1. Save your CapCut project as a template. Reuse the same caption style, color grade, and audio settings.

3. **Batch 3 (Reels 7-9)**: Speed and quality should both improve. By now, each reel should take 1.5-2 hours instead of 2.5-3 hours.

4. **Post-publish analysis**: After all 9 reels are live, check analytics:
   - Which reel got the most views? (Indicates best hook)
   - Which reel had the highest completion rate? (Indicates best pacing)
   - Which reel drove the most profile visits / WA clicks? (Indicates best CTA)
   - Use these insights to plan the next 9 reels.

5. **Build an asset library**: After producing these 9 reels, you will have:
   - ~30-50 usable clips of the real shop
   - Several Canva graphic templates
   - A voiceover recording setup
   - Tested BGM and SFX favorites
   - CapCut project templates

   This library makes the next batch of content 50% faster to produce.

6. **Audience feedback loop**: Ask customers in the shop: "Sudah lihat video kita di Instagram?" Their reactions tell you more than analytics. If they mention a specific reel, that one resonated. Make more like it.
