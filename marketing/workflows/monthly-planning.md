# Workflow: Monthly Marketing Planning

## Trigger
First week of each month (ideally Monday–Wednesday)

## Required Inputs
- Previous month's performance data (from Google Sheets / platform analytics)
- Seasonal calendar (upcoming events, holidays, school calendar)
- Current business objectives from owner
- Active campaign status
- Budget for upcoming month

## Steps

| # | Step | Owner Agent | Input | Output |
|---|------|-------------|-------|--------|
| 1 | Collect previous month performance data | **Analytics Optimizer** | Platform analytics, business metrics | Performance report with KPIs vs targets |
| 2 | Analyze top/bottom content and learnings | **Analytics Optimizer** | Content log, performance data | Insights report: what worked, what didn't, recommendations |
| 3 | Scan market/seasonal opportunities | **Market Research** | Seasonal calendar, competitor activity, trends | Opportunity brief: trends, seasonal angles, competitor moves |
| 4 | Set monthly objectives and strategy | **Strategy Planner** | Performance report, opportunity brief, business goals | Monthly objectives with measurable targets |
| 5 | Plan campaigns for the month | **Strategy Planner** | Objectives, seasonal calendar, promo schedule | Campaign briefs (new or continued) |
| 6 | Create 4-week content calendar | **Strategy Planner** + **Short-Form Content** | Objectives, campaigns, pillar mix targets | Filled content calendar with concepts per day |
| 7 | Set creative direction for the month | **Creative Director** | Campaigns, content calendar | Monthly creative direction brief |
| 8 | Review full plan for quality and feasibility | **Reviewer** | All outputs from steps 1–7 | Approval or revision requests |
| 9 | Compile and distribute final plan | **Orchestrator** | All approved outputs | Final monthly plan document |

## Review Gates

- **Gate 1** (after step 2): Analytics report reviewed by Strategy Planner before planning starts
- **Gate 2** (after step 6): Content calendar reviewed for pillar balance, volume feasibility, campaign coverage
- **Gate 3** (after step 8): Full plan reviewed by Reviewer — check all pricing, terms, campaign dates

## Required Outputs

- [ ] Monthly performance review document
- [ ] Monthly objectives with KPI targets
- [ ] Campaign briefs (if new campaigns)
- [ ] 4-week content calendar (concepts + pillars + platforms)
- [ ] Creative direction brief
- [ ] WA broadcast schedule (8 broadcasts)
- [ ] Google Maps posting schedule (4 posts)

## Completion Criteria

- All outputs approved by Reviewer
- Calendar has 24+ content concepts across 4 weeks
- Every campaign has a filled brief
- Owner has reviewed and approved objectives
- Week 1 content briefs are ready for weekly production workflow

## Notes

- If the month has a major seasonal event (Ramadan, Lebaran, 17 Agustus), plan the seasonal campaign first, then fill remaining calendar around it
- Monthly planning feeds into `workflows/weekly-content-production.md` for execution
- Archive previous month's plan in `calendar/` with date prefix
