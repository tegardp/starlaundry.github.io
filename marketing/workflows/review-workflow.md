# Workflow: Review / Quality Gate

## Trigger
Any deliverable is marked as ready for review by its originating agent.

## Required Inputs
- Deliverable to review (script, caption, WA broadcast, campaign plan, shot list, etc.)
- Source brief that the deliverable was created from
- Current pricing and promo terms (reference: website price list)
- Recent content log (to check for duplication)

## Steps

| # | Step | Owner Agent | Detail |
|---|------|-------------|--------|
| 1 | Receive deliverable | **Reviewer** | Log what's being reviewed, from which agent, linked to which brief |
| 2 | Brand voice check | **Reviewer** | Is the language casual Indonesian? No "Anda"? Friendly tone? |
| 3 | Platform fit check | **Reviewer** | Does it meet platform requirements? (length, caption format, hashtag count) |
| 4 | Accuracy check | **Reviewer** | Are prices, promo terms, hours, addresses correct? |
| 5 | Campaign alignment check | **Reviewer** | Does it match the campaign brief? Right message, right audience? |
| 6 | Duplication check | **Reviewer** | Is this too similar to content posted in the last 30 days? |
| 7 | CTA check | **Reviewer** | Does it include WhatsApp CTA? Is the number correct (0822-2567-2756)? |
| 8 | Quality check | **Reviewer** | Is the hook strong? Is the content valuable/entertaining? Would you stop scrolling? |
| 9 | Issue decision | **Reviewer** | APPROVE, REVISE (specific feedback), or REJECT (fundamental re-do) |
| 10 | Feedback delivery | **Reviewer** → originating agent | Specific, actionable feedback with exact fixes |
| 11 | Re-review (if revised) | **Reviewer** | Check that revisions address the feedback |
| 12 | Final approval | **Reviewer** → **Orchestrator** | Mark as approved, hand to Orchestrator for compilation |

## Review Checklists

### Script Review
- [ ] Hook works in 3 seconds — would you stop scrolling?
- [ ] Language: casual Indonesian, uses "Kak"/"kamu"
- [ ] No "Anda", no formal phrases
- [ ] WhatsApp CTA present with correct number
- [ ] Pricing accurate (check against: Cuci Lipat Rp 5,000, Cuci Setrika Rp 6,000, Self-Service Rp 10,000, Senin Hemat Rp 4,000 min 3kg, Cuci Pertama Rp 3,000)
- [ ] Duration realistic (15s/30s/45s/60s)
- [ ] Every beat has visual direction
- [ ] Alt hooks are genuinely different
- [ ] 15-sec version included
- [ ] Filmable with phone by 1–2 people
- [ ] Recording notes complete

### Caption Review
- [ ] Hook line compelling
- [ ] Matches approved script message
- [ ] WhatsApp CTA with correct number
- [ ] Pricing/promo accurate
- [ ] IG: 15–20 hashtags / TikTok: 3–5 hashtags
- [ ] Emoji moderate, not excessive
- [ ] Not duplicate of recent caption (<30 days)

### WA Broadcast Review
- [ ] Clear value (not just "promo!")
- [ ] Accurate pricing/terms
- [ ] Specific CTA
- [ ] Brand sign-off: "Star Laundry — Bersih Wangi, Pasti Rapi ⭐"
- [ ] Not 3rd+ broadcast this week (max 2)
- [ ] Emoji appropriate

### Campaign Plan Review
- [ ] Objectives are measurable
- [ ] Timeline: min 2 weeks lead (4 weeks for seasonal)
- [ ] Budget realistic
- [ ] Content volume achievable (max 7/week)
- [ ] Offers don't conflict with existing promos
- [ ] Channel mix covers WA + social + GMaps

## Feedback Format

When requesting revisions, use this format:
```
REVIEW RESULT: REVISE

Deliverable: [Script #047]
Agent: [Scriptwriter]

Issues:
1. ⚠️ [Specific issue] — [Exact fix needed]
   Line/section: [where]

2. ⚠️ [Specific issue] — [Exact fix needed]
   Line/section: [where]

Good:
- ✅ [What works well — keep this]
- ✅ [What works well]

Priority: [Fix issues 1-2 and resubmit]
```

## Completion Criteria
- Deliverable has passed all relevant checklist items
- Any revision requests have been addressed and re-reviewed
- Final status: APPROVED
- Approved deliverable handed to Orchestrator

## Review SLA
- Standard review: within 24 hours of submission
- Urgent review (marked by Orchestrator): within 4 hours
- Re-review (after revision): within 12 hours
