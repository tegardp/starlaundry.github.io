# Messaging Pillars — Star Laundry Boyolali

## Overview

Six core messaging pillars that form the foundation of all Star Laundry communication. Every piece of content, copy, or campaign should reinforce one or more of these pillars.

---

## Pillar 1: Peralatan Profesional (Professional Equipment)

**Core Message**: "Hasilnya beda karena alatnya beda"

**Supporting Points**:
- Mesin cuci 20kg — cucian longgar, tidak kusut, bersih maksimal
- Dryer profesional — 30 menit kering, tidak tergantung cuaca
- Setrika uap — hasil lebih rapi dibanding setrika biasa

**Proof Points**:
- Before/after visual comparison
- Machine size comparison (20kg vs 7kg rumahan)
- Steam iron close-up demonstration

**Sample Copy (Indonesian)**:
- "Mesin cuci rumah kamu 7kg. Punya kami 20kg. Hasilnya? Beda banget."
- "Setrika biasa vs setrika uap — lihat sendiri bedanya!"
- "Pakai dryer, 30 menit kering. Nggak pakai jemur, nggak pakai nunggu."

**When to Use**: Comparison content, educational content, overcoming "kenapa mahal" objection, first-time customer messaging

---

## Pillar 2: Kemudahan & Kenyamanan (Convenience)

**Core Message**: "Cuci beres tanpa repot"

**Supporting Points**:
- Antar jemput gratis (radius 3km, 3-5km min 5kg)
- Notifikasi otomatis via WhatsApp saat cucian selesai
- Self-service tersedia untuk yang mau cuci sendiri
- Buka setiap hari 07.00–21.00

**Proof Points**:
- Screenshot WhatsApp notification
- Delivery process video
- Self-service walkthrough

**Sample Copy (Indonesian)**:
- "Nggak perlu keluar rumah. Nggak perlu nanya 'sudah selesai belum'. Semuanya otomatis."
- "Antar jemput GRATIS! Tinggal WA, kami yang jemput cuciannya."
- "Buka tiap hari jam 7 pagi sampai 9 malam. Kapan aja mau cuci, kami siap."

**When to Use**: Targeting busy people (pekerja, keluarga), pickup/delivery promos, convenience comparison vs cuci sendiri

---

## Pillar 3: Harga Terjangkau (Affordability)

**Core Message**: "Laundry modern, harga merakyat"

**Supporting Points**:
- Cuci lipat mulai Rp 5,000/kg
- Self-service cuma Rp 10,000/7kg
- Promo regular: Senin Hemat Rp 4,000/kg
- Cuci pertama Rp 3,000/kg untuk pelanggan baru

**Proof Points**:
- Price comparison with value received
- "Rp 10,000 = 7kg bersih" visual
- Stamp card (10x cuci = 1x gratis)

**Sample Copy (Indonesian)**:
- "Mulai dari 5 ribu per kilo. Modern nggak harus mahal."
- "10 ribu doang — 7 kilo bersih, kering, wangi. Self service!"
- "Cuci pertama cuma 3 ribu/kg. Coba dulu, pasti ketagihan!"

**When to Use**: Student-targeted content, price objection handling, promo announcements, comparison with cost of doing laundry at home

---

## Pillar 4: Kecepatan (Speed)

**Core Message**: "Cepat selesai, nggak perlu nunggu lama"

**Supporting Points**:
- Cuci lipat 1 hari (standard)
- Express 6 jam dan 3 jam tersedia
- Self-service selesai 40 menit
- Dryer 30 menit (vs jemur berjam-jam)

**Proof Points**:
- Timer/clock visual showing speed
- Self-service process walkthrough (40 min total)
- Express service testimonial

**Sample Copy (Indonesian)**:
- "Pagi antar, sore jemput. Cuci lipat 1 hari selesai!"
- "Butuh buru-buru? Express 3 jam — bersih, kering, rapi."
- "Self service 40 menit selesai. Sambil nunggu, bisa ngopi dulu ☕"

**When to Use**: Express service promos, targeting pekerja kantoran, self-service content, urgency situations (interview besok, event weekend)

---

## Pillar 5: Kepercayaan & Garansi (Trust & Guarantee)

**Core Message**: "Cucian aman, kualitas terjamin"

**Supporting Points**:
- Garansi cuci ulang gratis jika tidak puas
- Labeling system — cucian tidak tertukar
- Checklist serah terima — jumlah potong dihitung
- Google Maps rating dan review positif

**Proof Points**:
- "Tidak puas? Kami cuci ulang GRATIS" banner/text
- Process video showing labeling system
- Google Maps review screenshots
- Customer testimonials

**Sample Copy (Indonesian)**:
- "Nggak puas? Kami cuci ulang GRATIS. Serius."
- "Setiap cucian dilabel — dijamin nggak tertukar."
- "Baca review kami di Google Maps — rata-rata bintang 4.5+ ⭐"

**When to Use**: First-time customer messaging, overcoming trust objection, social proof content, quality guarantee messaging

---

## Pillar 6: Pengalaman Modern (Modern Experience)

**Core Message**: "Laundry jaman sekarang ya begini"

**Supporting Points**:
- Koin laundry pertama di Boyolali
- Self-service tersedia (sebelumnya tidak ada di Boyolali)
- Sistem notifikasi WhatsApp otomatis
- Outlet bersih dan terang

**Proof Points**:
- Outlet tour video
- Self-service machine demo
- WA notification demo
- Before/after of laundry experience (traditional vs modern)

**Sample Copy (Indonesian)**:
- "Koin laundry pertama di Boyolali! Udah coba belum?"
- "Laundry jaman now — tinggal masukkan koin, 40 menit beres."
- "Masih cuci tangan? Ada cara yang lebih gampang lho..."

**When to Use**: Awareness-stage content, targeting people who haven't tried modern laundry, outlet tour content, differentiation from traditional
