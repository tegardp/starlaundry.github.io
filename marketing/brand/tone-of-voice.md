# Tone of Voice Guide — Star Laundry Boyolali

## Voice Characteristics

Star Laundry speaks like a **friendly neighbor who happens to be really good at laundry**. Not corporate. Not salesy. Helpful, practical, and warm.

| Characteristic | Level | Description |
|---------------|-------|-------------|
| **Friendly** | High | Like talking to a teman or tetangga — warm, approachable |
| **Practical** | High | Focus on solutions, results, and clear information |
| **Casual** | High | Conversational Indonesian, not formal or textbook |
| **Confident** | Medium | Proud of quality, but not arrogant |
| **Playful** | Medium | Light humor OK, but not clowning |
| **Formal** | Low | Avoid stiff, corporate, or bureaucratic language |

## Tone Spectrum

```
FORMAL ←————————————————→ CASUAL
         Star Laundry sits HERE ──→ ■■
```

We lean casual — but not sloppy. Think: friendly WhatsApp chat, not official letter.

## Language Rules

### Use (✅)
| Word/Phrase | Why |
|-------------|-----|
| "Kak" | Universal friendly address — works for all ages/genders |
| "kamu" | Casual "you" — appropriate for social media |
| "nggak" / "gak" | Natural casual negative — not "tidak" |
| "banget" | Casual intensifier — "bersih banget" |
| "yuk" | Friendly invitation — "Yuk cuci di Star Laundry" |
| "lho" / "loh" | Conversational emphasis — "Gratis lho!" |
| "dong" | Soft persuasion — "Coba dong" |
| "aja" | Casual — "WA aja" not "silakan WhatsApp" |
| "biar" | Purpose — "biar cucian wangi" |
| "sih" | Softener — "Kenapa sih baju bau?" |

### Avoid (❌)
| Word/Phrase | Why | Use Instead |
|-------------|-----|-------------|
| "Anda" | Too formal, creates distance | "Kak" or "kamu" |
| "Kami menawarkan" | Corporate speak | "Di Star Laundry ada..." |
| "Silakan" | Too formal | "Yuk" or "langsung aja" |
| "Dengan hormat" | Official letter tone | Just start talking |
| "Pelanggan yang terhormat" | Too stiff | "Kak" or "Halo!" |
| "Terima kasih atas kepercayaan Anda" | Generic formal | "Makasih ya, Kak! 🙏" |
| "Promo berlaku selama persediaan masih ada" | Legal boilerplate | "Promonya terbatas ya, Kak!" |

## Platform-Specific Tone

### WhatsApp
- **Tone**: Most personal and warm. Like chatting with a friend.
- **Format**: Short paragraphs, emoji bullets, clear structure
- **Sign-off**: Always end with "Star Laundry — Bersih Wangi, Pasti Rapi ⭐"
- **Example**:
  ```
  Halo Kak! 👋

  Hari ini hujan terus ya? 🌧
  Tenang, di Star Laundry pakai dryer — 30 menit kering, cuaca apapun! ☀

  Mau cuci hari ini? Balas chat ini aja ya 📲

  Star Laundry — Bersih Wangi, Pasti Rapi ⭐
  ```

### Instagram
- **Tone**: Friendly + slightly polished. Informative but still fun.
- **Format**: Hook line first, then body, then CTA, then hashtags
- **Example**:
  ```
  Musim hujan, cucian nggak kering-kering? 😩

  Di Star Laundry, pakai dryer profesional — 30 menit langsung kering,
  wangi, dan siap dilipat! Nggak perlu tunggu matahari ☀

  Cuci lipat mulai Rp 5.000/kg. Chat WA kami: 0822-2567-2756 📲

  #StarLaundry #LaundryBoyolali ...
  ```

### TikTok
- **Tone**: Most casual and punchy. Quick, relatable, slightly playful.
- **Format**: Very short captions, hook-driven, conversational
- **Example**:
  ```
  Masih jemur di luar pas hujan? 🌧 Ada cara lebih gampang sih...
  #LaundryBoyolali #StarLaundry #MusimHujan
  ```

### Google Maps
- **Tone**: Helpful and informative. Slightly more professional than social.
- **Format**: Short, clear, with key info
- **Example**:
  ```
  Promo Senin Hemat! Cuci lipat cuma Rp 4.000/kg (min 3kg).
  Buka setiap hari 07.00–21.00. Self service juga tersedia Rp 10.000/7kg!
  ```

## Emoji Usage Guide

| Context | Emoji | Usage |
|---------|-------|-------|
| Greeting | 👋 | Start of WA broadcasts |
| Quality/clean | ✨⭐ | Describing results |
| Action/CTA | 📲🔗 | WhatsApp links, bio links |
| Positive | ✅💪🎉 | Confirming, celebrating |
| Location | 📍 | Address mentions |
| Warning/attention | ⚠️❌ | "Don't do this" content |
| Price/deal | 💰🏷 | Promo announcements |
| Laundry-specific | 👕🧺 | Sparingly, for flavor |

**Rules**:
- 2–4 emoji per Instagram caption
- 4–6 emoji per WhatsApp message (more is OK)
- 1–2 emoji per TikTok caption
- 0–1 emoji per Google Maps post
- Never use emoji in the middle of a sentence — use at end of lines or as bullets

## On-Brand vs Off-Brand Examples

### Announcing a promo

**On-brand** ✅:
> Senin Hemat! 🎉 Cuci lipat cuma 4.000/kg (min 3kg). Hari ini aja ya, Kak! Chat WA kami: 0822-2567-2756 📲

**Off-brand** ❌:
> PROMO!!! SENIN HEMAT!!! DISKON GILA-GILAAN!!! CUCI SEKARANG JUGA!!! 🔥🔥🔥💥💥💥

### Responding to a complaint

**On-brand** ✅:
> Waduh, mohon maaf ya Kak 🙏 Boleh chat detail ke WA kami di 0822-2567-2756? Pasti kami bantu selesaikan.

**Off-brand** ❌:
> Terima kasih atas masukan Anda. Kami akan menindaklanjuti keluhan Anda sesuai prosedur yang berlaku.

### Educational content hook

**On-brand** ✅:
> Kamu tau nggak kenapa baju kamu bau padahal sudah dicuci?

**Off-brand** ❌:
> Tahukah Anda bahwa terdapat beberapa faktor yang menyebabkan pakaian berbau tidak sedap pasca pencucian?

### Describing the service

**On-brand** ✅:
> Tinggal antar cucian, nanti kami WA kalau sudah selesai. Gampang banget!

**Off-brand** ❌:
> Kami menyediakan layanan full service dengan notifikasi otomatis melalui WhatsApp untuk kenyamanan pelanggan.
