# Brand Positioning — Star Laundry Boyolali

## Positioning Statement

> **Star Laundry** adalah laundry modern pertama di Boyolali yang menggunakan mesin kapasitas besar (20kg), dryer profesional, dan setrika uap — menghasilkan cucian lebih bersih, wangi, dan rapi dibanding laundry tradisional. Tersedia self-service untuk yang mau hemat, dan full service untuk yang mau praktis.

## Taglines

| Tagline | Usage |
|---------|-------|
| **"Bersih Wangi, Pasti Rapi"** | Primary — use everywhere (logo, signage, WA sign-off, content) |
| "Laundry Modern Harga Merakyat" | Secondary — use when price is the main message |
| "Cuci Pakai Mesin Besar, Hasilnya Beda" | Tertiary — use in educational/comparison content |

## Value Proposition

**For** anak kos, pekerja kantoran, dan keluarga muda di Boyolali
**Who** butuh cucian bersih, wangi, dan rapi tanpa repot
**Star Laundry is** laundry modern dengan peralatan profesional
**That** menghasilkan cucian lebih baik dari laundry tradisional
**Unlike** laundry tradisional yang masih pakai jemur matahari dan setrika biasa
**We** pakai dryer profesional (tidak tergantung cuaca), setrika uap (lebih rapi), dan mesin 20kg (cucian tidak kusut)

## Competitive Positioning Matrix

| Aspect | Star Laundry | Laundry Tradisional |
|--------|-------------|-------------------|
| Pengeringan | Dryer profesional — 30 menit, cuaca apapun | Jemur matahari — tergantung cuaca, bisa bau |
| Setrika | Setrika uap — hasil lebih rapi, lebih cepat | Setrika listrik biasa |
| Kapasitas mesin | 20kg — cucian longgar, lebih bersih | Kecil/terbatas — cucian padat, kurang bersih |
| Notifikasi | Otomatis via WhatsApp | Manual / telepon / tidak ada |
| Self-service | Ada — Rp 10,000/7kg | Tidak ada |
| Antar jemput | Gratis (radius 3km) | Jarang / bayar ekstra |
| Harga | Rp 5,000–13,000/kg | Rp 4,000–7,000/kg (tapi kualitas beda) |
| Garansi | Cuci ulang gratis jika tidak puas | Jarang ada |

## Key Messages (wajib dikomunikasikan terus-menerus)

1. **"Pakai dryer, tidak tergantung cuaca"** — Differentiator #1, especially powerful during musim hujan
2. **"Setrika uap, hasilnya lebih rapi"** — Visual proof in content (before/after)
3. **"Mesin besar, cucian tidak kusut"** — Show the 20kg washer, compare to home machine
4. **"Notifikasi otomatis via WhatsApp"** — Convenience, modern service
5. **"Mulai dari Rp 5,000/kg"** — Affordability despite better quality
6. **"Antar jemput GRATIS"** — Removes barrier for trying the service

## Brand Personality

| Trait | Description | In Practice |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| **Modern** | First coin laundry in Boyolali, professional equipment | Clean visuals, tech touches (WA notifications) |
| **Friendly** | Neighborhood business, personal touch | Call customers "Kak", remember names, casual tone |
| **Practical** | Solve real problems, no fluff | Focus on results (bersih, wangi, rapi), clear pricing |
| **Reliable** | Consistent quality, on-time delivery | Guarantee (cuci ulang gratis), transparent timing |
| **Affordable** | Modern quality at accessible prices | "Harga merakyat", show value comparisons |

## Positioning Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Lead with equipment superiority (dryer, steam iron, 20kg washer) — this is the core differentiator
- Show results visually (before/after, close-ups of clean folded laundry)
- Emphasize convenience (pickup/delivery, WA notifications, fast turnaround)
- Use real pricing — transparency builds trust
- Compare against sun-drying/traditional methods (this is the competitive frame)
- Highlight the guarantee (cuci ulang gratis)

### Don't
- Don't position as "premium" or "luxury" — position as "modern at accessible prices"
- Don't attack traditional laundry by name — compare methods, not businesses
- Don't use English-heavy copy — stay in natural Indonesian
- Don't promise what you can't deliver (realistic turnaround times)
- Don't hide pricing — price transparency is a trust builder
- Don't over-polish content to look "corporate" — stay friendly and neighborhood-feel
